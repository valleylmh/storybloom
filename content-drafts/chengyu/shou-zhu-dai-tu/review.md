## 逐页文字

1. 从前，宋国有个年轻农夫。每天太阳刚升起，他就下田干活。
   Long ago in the state of Song, a young farmer worked his field from sunrise.
2. 田边有个老树桩。农夫累了，就坐在旁边喝口水。
   An old stump stood by the field. Whenever he grew tired, he rested beside it with a drink of water.
3. 一天，一只野兔匆匆跑来，咚地撞上树桩，晕了过去。
   One day, a wild rabbit rushed past and bumped into the stump. It tumbled down, dazed.
4. 农夫抱起兔子，惊喜地想：“不耕田，也能有收获呀！”
   The farmer gently picked up the rabbit and thought, “A harvest without work!”
5. 第二天，他放下锄头，坐在树桩旁，盼着兔子再来。
   The next morning, he set down his hoe and waited by the stump for another rabbit.
6. 一天又一天，兔子没有出现，田里的禾苗却被杂草围住了。
   Day after day, no rabbit came. Meanwhile, weeds crowded around the neglected crops.
7. 望着荒下来的田，他终于明白：守株待兔，等不来真正的收获。
   Looking at his untended field, he finally understood: waiting by a stump could never bring a true harvest.
8. 他重新拿起锄头，认真耕种。秋天，田野铺满了金黄。
   He picked up his hoe and worked with care again. By autumn, the field glowed gold.
