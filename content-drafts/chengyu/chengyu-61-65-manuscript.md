# 成语与谚语 61–65：确认版故事正文

## 61. 三个臭皮匠，顶个诸葛亮｜会转弯的小花车
常用谚语；依据谚语寓意创作的原创童话，并非历史典故。
寓意：好点子不只来自一个人。愿意倾听、一起试验，能把不同的想法变成共同的办法。

### 第 1 页
村里的花车游行快到了，小猪、小鸭和小刺猬决定一起造辆小花车。
The village parade was coming. Piglet, Duckling, and Hedgehog decided to build a flower wagon together.

### 第 2 页
小猪搬来木板，小鸭画好花样，小刺猬把零件摆得整整齐齐。
Piglet brought the boards, Duckling drew the flowers, and Hedgehog arranged the parts in neat rows.

### 第 3 页
他们请木匠伯伯帮忙，装好车板和轮子，再系上漂亮的纸花。
With the carpenter’s help, they fitted the deck and wheels, then tied on bright paper flowers.

### 第 4 页
“出发！”小猪轻轻推车，纸花在阳光下晃呀晃。
“Here we go!” Piglet pushed gently, and the paper flowers bobbed in the sunshine.

### 第 5 页
到了石墙边的窄弯，车头过去了，长长的车身却转不过去。
At a tight bend between stone walls, the front slipped through, but the long deck could not turn.

### 第 6 页
小猪又推了一下，车轮还是不动。“先停停！”小刺猬喊道。
Piglet pushed once more, but the wheels stayed still. “Let’s stop!” called Hedgehog.

### 第 7 页
小鸭说：“换条宽路吧！”可那条路正挖沟，今天不能走。
“Let’s take the wider road,” said Duckling. But that road was being repaired and was closed for the day.

### 第 8 页
小刺猬提议把车做短一些。小猪却舍不得拆掉刚装好的车板。
Hedgehog suggested a shorter wagon. Piglet did not want to take apart the deck they had just finished.

### 第 9 页
“我的办法最好！”“我的也有用！”三个人越说越急。
“My idea is best!” “Mine could work too!” Their voices grew louder and louder.

### 第 10 页
小鸭垂下翅膀：“我们都在说，可谁也没听清。”
Duckling lowered her wings. “We’re all talking, but none of us is really listening.”

### 第 11 页
他们坐到树下，约定轮流说：想改什么，为什么，又担心什么。
They sat beneath a tree and took turns explaining their ideas, reasons, and worries.

### 第 12 页
小猪说：“我希望车结实。”小刺猬说：“我希望它转得过弯。”
“I want it to be strong,” said Piglet. “I want it to get around the corner,” said Hedgehog.

### 第 13 页
小鸭在地上画出弯道：“先做一辆小小的，试试看吧。”
Duckling drew the bend on the ground. “Let’s try a tiny model first.”

### 第 14 页
他们用积木搭石墙，用小纸盒当花车。纸盒果然也卡住了。
They made walls from blocks and used a small cardboard box as the wagon. It got stuck too.

### 第 15 页
小刺猬换上短一点的纸盒。车身能挪动了，可轮子还不肯转弯。
Hedgehog tried a shorter box. There was more room now, but the wheels still would not steer.

### 第 16 页
小鸭拨动前轮：“要是这两个轮子能一起转向呢？”
Duckling nudged the front wheels. “What if these two wheels could turn together?”

### 第 17 页
小猪找来小圆片和木棒。他们做出能转向的前轴，再试了一遍。
Piglet found a small disc and a wooden rod. Together they made a turning front axle and tried again.

### 第 18 页
小模型顺利绕过弯道！三个脑袋挤在一起，笑出了声。
The tiny wagon went around the bend! Three heads leaned close, and three happy laughs rang out.

### 第 19 页
木匠伯伯照着模型帮忙改车：车板短一些，前轮也能转向了。
The carpenter helped them follow the model: a shorter deck and front wheels that could steer.

### 第 20 页
这回到了窄弯，小猪慢推，小鸭引路，小刺猬留意车轮。
This time Piglet pushed slowly, Duckling guided the way, and Hedgehog watched the wheels.

### 第 21 页
花车稳稳地转了过去，大家一起欢呼：“我们做到啦！”
The wagon rolled smoothly around the bend. “We did it!” they cheered together.

### 第 22 页
游行中，三位朋友并肩走着。小花车上，装着他们一起想出的好办法。
In the parade, the three friends walked side by side. Their little wagon carried an idea they had made together.

## 62. 磨刀不误砍柴工｜小兔的剪纸灯会
常用谚语；依据谚语寓意创作的原创童话，并非历史典故。
寓意：准备、练习和检查工具都值得花时间。找到合适的方法，再耐心动手，事情会更顺利。

### 第 1 页
今晚村里要办灯会，小兔想剪一百颗纸星星，把院子装扮得亮亮的。
The village lantern evening was coming. Rabbit wanted to cut one hundred paper stars to brighten the courtyard.

### 第 2 页
她抱来一大摞彩纸，刚坐稳，就拿起剪刀咔嚓咔嚓剪起来。
She brought a tall stack of colored paper and began snipping as soon as she sat down.

### 第 3 页
第一颗星星少了一个角，第二颗又瘦又歪，第三颗一打开就散了。
The first star lost a point. The second was thin and crooked. The third fell apart when she unfolded it.

### 第 4 页
地上的碎纸越来越多，桌上的好星星却只有两颗。
More and more scraps covered the floor, but only two good stars lay on the table.

### 第 5 页
小老鼠来帮忙，看见小兔皱着眉，便轻声问：“哪里不顺手呀？”
Mouse came to help. Seeing Rabbit frown, he gently asked, “Which part is giving you trouble?”

### 第 6 页
“剪刀总夹纸！”小兔使劲捏了一下，纸边又被扯破了。
“The scissors keep catching!” Rabbit squeezed harder, and the edge of the paper tore again.

### 第 7 页
妈妈走过来：“先把剪刀放下，我们一起看看。”
Mother came over. “Put the scissors down first. Let’s take a look together.”

### 第 8 页
原来刀口粘着干胶。妈妈清理、检查后，又试剪了一小条纸。
Dried glue had stuck to the blades. Mother cleaned and checked them, then tested them on a strip of paper.

### 第 9 页
小兔望着窗外：“准备这么久，星星会不会来不及呀？”
Rabbit looked out of the window. “If we spend so long getting ready, will we finish in time?”

### 第 10 页
“先试出一个好办法。”妈妈递来纸板，让小兔画一颗满意的星星。
“Let’s find a method that works.” Mother offered cardboard for Rabbit to draw a star she liked.

### 第 11 页
小兔改了两次，终于画好。妈妈帮她把硬纸板剪成了样板。
Rabbit tried twice until she liked the shape. Mother helped cut the stiff cardboard into a template.

### 第 12 页
小老鼠扶住样板，小兔沿着边描，一颗清楚的星星出现在彩纸上。
Mouse held the template still while Rabbit traced around it. A clear star appeared on the colored paper.

### 第 13 页
这一次，小兔慢慢剪，转动纸张，一颗完整的星星落在手心里。
This time Rabbit cut slowly and turned the paper. A whole star settled into her palm.

### 第 14 页
“成啦！”她没有马上抢着多剪，而是先把纸、样板和小筐摆好。
“It worked!” Instead of rushing ahead, she arranged the paper, template, and small baskets first.

### 第 15 页
小老鼠描线，小兔剪纸，妈妈把剪好的星星穿在棉线上。
Mouse traced, Rabbit cut, and Mother threaded the finished stars onto cotton string.

### 第 16 页
剪到手酸时，他们停下来伸伸胳膊，喝口水，再接着做。
When their hands grew tired, they stretched, drank some water, and then carried on.

### 第 17 页
小兔发现，不用一百颗也很好看。她把星星疏疏地排开，留下灯光的位置。
Rabbit discovered that fewer than one hundred looked lovely too. She spaced the stars apart, leaving room for the lights.

### 第 18 页
太阳快落山时，星星挂好了。微风一吹，金的、红的、蓝的轻轻摇晃。
By sunset the stars were hanging. Gold, red, and blue shapes swayed gently in the breeze.

### 第 19 页
灯亮起来，小兔拉着小老鼠钻进星光下面：“这里还有你画的那颗！”
The lights came on. Rabbit led Mouse beneath the stars. “There’s the one you traced!”

### 第 20 页
妈妈说：“磨刀不误砍柴工。”小兔笑了：“原来准备好，也是往前走。”
“Good preparation helps the work along,” said Mother. Rabbit smiled. “Getting ready is a way of moving forward too.”

## 63. 远水救不了近火｜小兔花园里的急事
常用谚语；依据谚语寓意创作的原创童话，并非历史典故。
寓意：遇到急事，先寻找安全、及时、能解决眼前问题的办法，再为以后做好准备。

### 第 1 页
小兔把花苗移进了新花坛，盼着夏天开出一片粉红色的小花。
Rabbit moved young plants into a new garden bed, hoping for a summer full of pink flowers.

### 第 2 页
第二天下午，太阳火辣辣的。花苗低着头，叶子软软地垂下来。
The next afternoon was very hot. The seedlings bowed their heads, their soft leaves drooping.

### 第 3 页
小兔摸摸泥土：“干了，得给它们浇水！”
Rabbit touched the soil. “It’s dry. They need water!”

### 第 4 页
可花园里的水缸见了底，水龙头也因为修管子暂时停水。
But the garden water jar was empty, and the tap was off while the village pipes were being repaired.

### 第 5 页
“山上的泉水最清！”小兔提起水壶，“我去打一壶回来。”
“The spring in the hills has the clearest water!” Rabbit lifted the watering can. “I’ll fetch some.”

### 第 6 页
小松鼠拦在门边：“那里很远呀，走过去再回来，要好久呢。”
Squirrel paused at the gate. “That’s a long way. It will take ages to go there and back.”

### 第 7 页
小兔看看山，又看看垂着头的花苗，脚步慢了下来。
Rabbit looked at the hills, then at the drooping plants, and slowed to a stop.

### 第 8 页
“先找附近能用的水吧。”小松鼠说，“还可以想办法遮遮太阳。”
“Let’s look for water nearby first,” said Squirrel. “And find some shade for them.”

### 第 9 页
他们请山羊阿姨帮忙。阿姨先看了看花苗，又摸了摸土。
They asked Goat for help. She checked the seedlings and gently felt the soil.

### 第 10 页
“先搭个遮阳棚，让它们少晒一会儿。”阿姨从屋里取来浅色布。
“Let’s give them shade first.” Goat brought a pale cloth from her house.

### 第 11 页
木杆插稳了，布棚撑起来了。棚顶高高的，四周都能透风。
The stakes stood firmly, and the cloth went up. It was high enough for air to flow beneath it.

### 第 12 页
小兔想起厨房里还有一小壶干净的存水，赶紧和妈妈说了花园的事。
Rabbit remembered a small jug of clean stored water in the kitchen and told Mother about the garden.

### 第 13 页
妈妈留好家里要喝的水，把能分出来的那一点倒进小水壶。
Mother set aside the water the family needed to drink and poured a little extra into the watering can.

### 第 14 页
山羊阿姨扶着壶嘴，小兔慢慢浇，让水落在根边的泥土上。
Goat guided the spout as Rabbit watered slowly, letting the water soak into the soil around the roots.

### 第 15 页
水不多，他们先照顾最缺水的几株，再把剩下的分给其他花苗。
There was not much water. They tended the driest plants first, then shared the rest among the others.

### 第 16 页
“还没有抬头呢。”小兔蹲着等。阿姨说：“先让它们缓一缓。”
“They’re still drooping,” said Rabbit, waiting beside them. “Give them time to recover,” said Goat.

### 第 17 页
傍晚凉快了，有几片叶子慢慢挺起来。小兔终于松了一口气。
As the evening cooled, a few leaves began to lift. Rabbit finally let out a relieved breath.

### 第 18 页
“远水救不了近火。”阿姨说，“今天先用上了来得及的办法。”
“Distant water cannot put out a nearby fire,” said Goat. “Today we found help that could arrive in time.”

### 第 19 页
水管修好后，小兔请妈妈准备了带盖的储水桶，还画了一张浇水提醒图。
When the pipes were fixed, Rabbit and Mother set up a covered water barrel and made a watering reminder picture.

### 第 20 页
过了些日子，小花终于开了。花坛旁的小水壶，已经装好了明早要用的水。
Some days later, the flowers finally bloomed. Beside the bed, the little can was ready for the next morning.

## 64. 路遥知马力，日久见人心｜一路上的好朋友
常用谚语；依据谚语寓意创作的原创童话，并非历史典故。
寓意：了解朋友需要时间。热情的话语和安静的行动都可能表达关心，也要学习主动关心别人。

### 第 1 页
山上的小树屋要变成图书角了，小鹿报名帮忙，每周送一箱书上去。
The little treehouse on the hill was becoming a library. Deer volunteered to carry up a box of books each week.

### 第 2 页
小狐狸和小獾也来了。小狐狸笑着说：“一路有我，肯定不闷！”
Fox and Badger joined him. “With me along, the journey won’t be dull!” said Fox with a grin.

### 第 3 页
小獾只点点头，蹲下来，把箱子上的绳结重新系紧。
Badger simply nodded, crouched down, and tightened the knot around a box.

### 第 4 页
小鹿心想：小狐狸真热情，小獾是不是不太愿意和我玩呢？
Deer thought Fox seemed friendly. He wondered whether quiet Badger wanted to be friends at all.

### 第 5 页
第一次上山，小狐狸讲起蘑菇伞的笑话，小鹿笑得差点忘了脚下的坡。
On their first climb, Fox told a joke about a mushroom umbrella. Deer laughed and nearly forgot the uphill path.

### 第 6 页
小獾走在后面，不时提醒：“前面有树根，慢一点。”
Badger followed behind, occasionally saying, “There’s a root ahead. Take it slowly.”

### 第 7 页
到了树屋，他们把书摆上架。小鹿忙着说笑，没看见小獾把空箱折好了。
At the treehouse they shelved the books. Busy chatting, Deer did not notice Badger folding the empty boxes.

### 第 8 页
第二周，箱子装得多了一点。走到半路，小鹿的绳结突然松开了。
The next week their boxes were a little fuller. Halfway up, the cord around Deer’s box suddenly slipped loose.

### 第 9 页
几本书滑到草地上。小鹿红着脸：“糟了，都怪我没系好。”
A few books slid onto the grass. Deer blushed. “Oh no. I didn’t tie it properly.”

### 第 10 页
小狐狸先蹲下捡书：“别急，书都在，我们一起收。”
Fox knelt to gather the books. “Don’t worry. They’re all here. Let’s pick them up together.”

### 第 11 页
小獾拿出备用绳，把箱底托牢，还教小鹿试着打了一个结。
Badger took out spare cord, supported the box, and helped Deer practice a secure knot.

### 第 12 页
重新出发时，小鹿主动等了等：“小獾，走到我旁边来吧。”
When they set off again, Deer waited. “Badger, come and walk beside me.”

### 第 13 页
第三周下过雨，路边多了几个水洼。小狐狸的话比平时少了。
Rain had fallen before their third trip, leaving puddles beside the path. Fox was quieter than usual.

### 第 14 页
小鹿回过头，发现小狐狸的鞋带松了，一只鞋还进了小石子。
Deer looked back and saw Fox’s loose shoelace. A tiny stone had slipped into one shoe too.

### 第 15 页
“先歇一会儿。”小鹿接过他的箱子，小獾找了一块干燥的地方。
“Let’s rest.” Deer took Fox’s box, and Badger found a dry place to sit.

### 第 16 页
小狐狸倒出石子，重新系好鞋带。三个人坐着分吃带来的小饼。
Fox shook out the stone and retied his shoe. The three friends sat and shared their little biscuits.

### 第 17 页
小鹿这才发现，爱说笑的朋友也会累，安静的朋友也有好多话想说。
Deer began to see that a cheerful friend could get tired, and a quiet friend could have plenty to say.

### 第 18 页
小獾说，他最喜欢讲星星的书。小狐狸立刻说：“下次你讲给我们听！”
Badger said he loved books about stars. “Tell us about them next time!” said Fox.

### 第 19 页
一周又一周，树屋的书架渐渐满了。他们也越来越懂得照顾彼此。
Week after week, the shelves filled up. The friends grew better at looking after one another too.

### 第 20 页
有时小狐狸带路，有时小獾讲故事，有时小鹿提醒大家停下来喝水。
Sometimes Fox led, sometimes Badger told stories, and sometimes Deer reminded everyone to stop for water.

### 第 21 页
图书角开放那天，三位朋友坐在一起。小鹿说：“幸好这一路有你们。”
On opening day, the three sat together in the library. “I’m glad you were with me all the way,” said Deer.

### 第 22 页
回家的路还很长，小鹿却不着急。他知道，好朋友是在日子里慢慢认识的。
The walk home was still long, but Deer was in no hurry. Good friends, he knew, were discovered over time.

## 65. 一寸光阴一寸金，寸金难买寸光阴｜小猴的日落约定
常用谚语；依据谚语寓意创作的原创童话，并非历史典故。
寓意：时间珍贵，尤其是与人相处的时光。给重要的约定留出时间，也能安心享受玩耍。

### 第 1 页
清早，小乌龟告诉小猴：“我们今天搬家，太阳落山前就出发。”
That morning Turtle told Monkey, “We’re moving today. We’ll leave before sunset.”

### 第 2 页
小猴有点舍不得：“我给你画张像吧，把咱们的小池塘也画进去！”
Monkey felt sad to say goodbye. “I’ll draw your portrait, with our little pond in it!”

### 第 3 页
小乌龟笑了：“那我们下午一起画，我还能帮你挑颜色。”
Turtle smiled. “Let’s draw together this afternoon. I can help you choose the colors.”

### 第 4 页
小猴把纸和画笔摆好，抬头看看太阳：“还早着呢，先玩一会儿。”
Monkey set out paper and pencils, then looked up at the sun. “There’s lots of time. I’ll play a little first.”

### 第 5 页
一只蓝蝴蝶飞过篱笆，小猴跟着跑到草地上，看它停在花瓣间。
A blue butterfly flew over the fence. Monkey followed it to the meadow and watched it settle on a flower.

### 第 6 页
草地真好玩。他滚了两圈，又捡了一颗圆圆的小石子。
The meadow was fun. He rolled twice in the grass and found a little round pebble.

### 第 7 页
吃过午饭，小猴想起画像，刚走到桌边，又听见池塘边的水声。
After lunch Monkey remembered the portrait. He reached the table, then heard splashing by the pond.

### 第 8 页
他坐在浅浅的岸边，用叶子做小船，一放就是好几只。
He sat safely at the shallow edge and floated leaf boats, one after another.

### 第 9 页
小乌龟来找他：“现在画吗？”小猴说：“等这只船漂到那边就来。”
“Shall we draw now?” asked Turtle. “As soon as this boat reaches the other side,” said Monkey.

### 第 10 页
可小船绕着水草打转。等小猴抬起头，小乌龟已经回去帮妈妈了。
But the boat circled around the reeds. When Monkey looked up, Turtle had gone home to help Mother.

### 第 11 页
树影越来越长。村口传来收拾行李的声音，小猴一下子站起来。
The tree shadows grew longer. Monkey heard bundles being packed near the gate and suddenly stood up.

### 第 12 页
桌上的画纸还是空白的。小猴握住画笔，心里又急又难过。
The paper on the table was still blank. Monkey held a pencil, feeling hurried and sad.

### 第 13 页
他跑到小乌龟家：“对不起，我让你等了。我们现在还能一起画吗？”
He ran to Turtle’s house. “I’m sorry I kept you waiting. Can we still draw together?”

### 第 14 页
小乌龟摇摇头：“我得帮妈妈检查东西了，不过，我还是想收到你的画。”
Turtle shook his head. “I have to help Mother check our things. But I’d still love your drawing.”

### 第 15 页
小猴回到桌前，没有再想着画满整张纸。他先画朋友弯弯的笑眼。
Monkey returned to the table. He stopped trying to fill the whole page and began with his friend’s smiling eyes.

### 第 16 页
接着是圆圆的龟壳、小池塘，还有他们常坐的那片绿草地。
Next came the round shell, the pond, and the grassy spot where they often sat together.

### 第 17 页
画好了。小猴仔细夹好画纸，赶在出发前送到小乌龟手里。
The picture was finished. Monkey protected it between two boards and brought it to Turtle before they left.

### 第 18 页
“这里少了一根芦苇。”小乌龟笑着指出来。小猴递过绿笔，让他添上。
“It needs one reed here,” Turtle said with a smile. Monkey offered a green pencil so he could add it.

### 第 19 页
小猴陪他们走到村口。这一次，他认真听朋友说新家的模样。
Monkey walked with the family to the village gate. This time he listened carefully as Turtle described their new home.

### 第 20 页
夕阳照着画像，小猴轻轻挥手。他记住了：重要的约定，要留出真正相处的时间。
Sunset warmed the picture as Monkey waved goodbye. He would remember to leave real time for the promises and people he cared about.
