# 日常系列第 51 本逐页文稿与分镜（周三值日生与家长志愿者）

用户于 2026-10-27 确认：周三安安当值日生，妈妈来园做志愿者站岗；结尾为妈妈在岗、安安在园内远远打招呼。

## 今天妈妈在校门口站岗

副标题：一个值日生和一个志愿者

寓意：帮大家做事的位置有很多种；在自己的岗位上做好小事，就是在为大家站岗。

亲子目标：理解“值日”和“志愿服务”都是为集体做事，体验在自己的位置上完成责任的成就感，同时学习在成人工作时保持安全距离。

家长提醒：不把站岗说成“妈妈在看着你”，不让孩子因家长做志愿者而觉得自己特殊；也不鼓励孩子为了找妈妈离开班级队伍或跑出安全范围。

### 第 1 页

星期三的早晨，幼儿园门口的宣传栏上多了一张新的排班表，上面写着“家长志愿者护学岗”。安安一眼就看见了妈妈的名字。

On Wednesday morning a new roster appeared on the notice board by the kindergarten gate: “Parent Volunteer Gate Duty.” An'an spotted Mom's name right away.

分镜：Wide sunny gate view: wooden notice board with a plain colored grid of blank slots (no letters), Mom in coral cardigan pointing at it, An'an in teal hoodie and orange backpack on tiptoe.

### 第 2 页

“今天下午四点，妈妈要在门口当志愿者，穿黄马甲，帮大家慢慢走、看好路口。”妈妈蹲下来，把折好的黄马甲给她看。

“This afternoon at four, I'll volunteer at the gate in a yellow vest, helping everyone walk slowly and watching the crossing,” Mom said, kneeling to show An'an the folded vest in her tote bag.

分镜：Eye-level two-shot on clean gate steps: Mom kneels holding a plain folded yellow vest; An'an looks at it with curious bright eyes.

### 第 3 页

安安问：“站岗是不是就像我当值日生？”妈妈笑了：“有点像，都是在自己的位置上，帮大家做点小事。”

An'an asked, “Is gate duty like being the class helper?” Mom smiled. “A little. Both mean helping everyone from your own spot.”

分镜：Warm close two-shot: An'an tilting her head with a thinking smile; Mom answering with a gentle nod, morning light behind them.

### 第 4 页

走进大班教室，林老师正在更新值日表：“今天是周三，轮到安安值日啦。”印着金色小星的红袖标套上了安安的左胳膊。

Inside the senior classroom, Teacher Lin was updating the duty chart: “It's Wednesday, so An'an is our helper today.” A red armband with a gold star went onto An'an's left sleeve.

分镜：Classroom morning view: Teacher Lin fastens red star armband onto An'an's teal hoodie sleeve; Tiantian watches happily from a low wooden shelf.

### 第 5 页

第一件事是整理阅读角。安安把绘本按大小一本一本排好，把翘起来的书角轻轻压平。

The first job was the reading corner. An'an lined the picture books up by size and gently smoothed the curled corners.

分镜：Reading corner view: An'an kneeling at a low wooden shelf, arranging blank-cover picture books by height, small hands pressing a curled corner flat.

### 第 6 页

她发现一本书里夹着一张折成小方块的画，就把它交给林老师：“这不是书里的，可能是谁掉的。”

She found a small folded drawing tucked inside one book and brought it to Teacher Lin. “This isn't part of the book. Someone may have lost it.”

分镜：Close view: An'an holds a small folded paper with simple abstract shapes beside an open picture book; Teacher Lin reaches to take it.

### 第 7 页

第二件事是叠午睡用的小毛巾。毛巾筐放在高架子上，安安踮起脚，还是够不到。

The second job was folding the small nap towels. The basket sat high on a shelf, and even on tiptoe An'an could not reach it.

分镜：Medium shot: An'an stretching on tiptoe with heels on the floor, fingertips short of a wicker basket on a high shelf; no stool nearby.

### 第 8 页

她没有搬凳子往上爬，而是去请林老师帮忙：“老师，这个太高了，我够不着。”老师把毛巾筐取了下来。

She did not drag a stool over to climb. Instead she asked Teacher Lin, “It's too high for me — could you help?” The teacher lifted the basket down.

分镜：Two-person view: An'an speaking to Teacher Lin with an open honest face; Teacher Lin reaching up to lift the wicker basket down safely.

### 第 9 页

安安把毛巾一条一条叠成小方块，边角对整齐，再轻轻放进筐里。

An'an folded the towels one by one into neat squares, matching the corners, and set them gently in the basket.

分镜：Tabletop close-up: small hands folding a soft towel into a tidy square, a neat stack already finished beside a wicker basket.

### 第 10 页

天天凑过来小声问：“安安，你妈妈下午真的会来站岗吗？”安安点点头，心里也开始盼着四点。

Tiantian leaned in and whispered, “An'an, is your mom really coming for gate duty this afternoon?” An'an nodded, already looking forward to four o'clock.

分镜：Two children at the folding table: Tiantian in sky-blue sweatshirt whispering; An'an nodding with a hopeful smile, towel stack between them.

### 第 11 页

下午户外活动结束，安安在二楼走廊的窗边往下看。大门口的路边，一个穿黄马甲的身影正站在那里。

When outdoor play ended, An'an looked down from the second-floor corridor window. By the road at the gate stood a figure in a yellow vest.

分镜：Interior corridor window view looking down onto the sunny gate road: a small figure in a plain yellow vest standing at the crossing; An'an's hands on the sill.

### 第 12 页

是妈妈。妈妈举起手，示意过往的车慢一点，又弯下腰，牵着一个小朋友走过通道。

It was Mom. She raised a hand to signal the passing cars to slow down, then bent low to lead a small child across the walkway.

分镜：Gate-level medium shot: Mom in plain yellow vest over coral cardigan raising one hand to traffic, other hand holding a younger child's hand on a plain crosswalk.

### 第 13 页

安安刚想跑下楼，又停住了。她想起自己还在值日，玩具筐还没有收完。

An'an was about to run downstairs, then stopped. She remembered she was still on duty, and the toy baskets were not put away yet.

分镜：Corridor shot: An'an mid-step with one hand on the stair railing, glancing back toward her classroom door, caught between two choices.

### 第 14 页

她跑去问林老师：“我可以到窗边跟妈妈挥挥手吗？”老师说：“可以，就站在窗边，不离开队伍。”

She ran to ask Teacher Lin, “May I wave to Mom from the window?” The teacher said, “Yes — stand right at the window, and stay with our group.”

分镜：Two-person corridor view: An'an asking with a small hopeful gesture; Teacher Lin nodding and pointing to the corridor window, children waiting in line behind.

### 第 15 页

安安把小手贴在玻璃上，用力挥了挥。妈妈抬起头，也回了一个小小的挥手，然后继续看着路口。

An'an pressed her hand to the glass and waved hard. Mom looked up, waved back just once, then turned her eyes to the crossing again.

分镜：Framed window view from inside: An'an's palm flat on the glass, waving; outside and below, Mom glances up with a brief smile and raised hand.

### 第 16 页

妈妈没有走上来抱她。安安的鼻子有点酸，可看到妈妈身后的队伍排得整整齐齐，她又觉得心里暖暖的。

Mom did not come upstairs to hug her. An'an's nose tingled a little, but seeing the tidy line of children behind Mom, her heart felt warm again.

分镜：Close portrait of An'an at the window with slightly wistful eyes; beyond the glass, a neat queue of children and Mom small at the crossing.

### 第 17 页

收好最后一只玩具筐，安安把红袖标交回老师手里。林老师在她手心贴了一颗金色小星星：“今天的值日生，站好了自己的岗。”

With the last toy basket stored away, An'an returned the red armband. Teacher Lin pressed a small gold star into her palm: “Today's helper stood her post well.”

分镜：Classroom late-afternoon view: Teacher Lin placing a gold foil star in An'an's open palm; red armband resting neatly on the desk, tidy shelves behind.

### 第 18 页

放学时，安安排队走到门口。妈妈还站在岗位上，等最后一个小朋友被接走，才脱下黄马甲，蹲下来抱住她：“今天，我们都在自己的位置上帮了忙。”

At dismissal, An'an's line reached the gate. Mom stayed at her post until the last child was picked up, then took off the yellow vest and knelt to hug her: “Today we both helped from where we stood.”

分镜：Warm golden closing shot at the gate: Mom kneeling with open arms and folded yellow vest over her arm; An'an running into the hug, orange backpack bouncing, last children leaving hand in hand.
