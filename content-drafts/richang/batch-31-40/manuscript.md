## 妈妈，你是在凶我吗

副标题：声音轻一点，我听得更清楚

寓意：可以说出被大声音吓到的感受；大人也可以放低声音，重新把事情说清楚。

亲子目标：让孩子辨认声音带来的感受，成人承担调节语气和修复沟通的责任。

家长提醒：不要求孩子忍受吼叫来证明懂事，也不以大人着急为伤人语气开脱。紧急危险由成人先保护安全，事后仍要说明。

### 第 1 页

周末出门前，安安正给小车找一个停车位。妈妈已经拿好了帆布包。

Before their weekend outing, An'an was finding a parking spot for her toy car. Mom already had the canvas bag.

分镜：Wide sunny home entryway: An'an crouches beside a small red toy car and oak shoe bench; Mom waits holding a plain cream canvas bag.

### 第 2 页

小车刚停好，又滑出来一点。安安蹲下来，想把它放得更稳。

The car rolled out a little. An'an crouched down to put it somewhere steadier.

分镜：Low close view: An'an gently steadies the red toy car beside the shoe bench, concentrating; only An'an visible.

### 第 3 页

妈妈看了看钟，声音一下变大了：“安安，快一点！我们要出门啦！”

Mom looked at the clock, and her voice suddenly grew loud. “An'an, hurry! It's time to go!”

分镜：Medium entryway: Mom calls with tense shoulders while holding canvas bag; An'an looks up startled beside red toy car; no angry pointing.

### 第 4 页

安安的手停在半空。肩膀缩了起来，刚才想说的话也缩了回去。

An'an's hand stopped in midair. Her shoulders drew inward, and the words she wanted to say seemed to hide too.

分镜：Close portrait: An'an's hand pauses above red toy car, shoulders drawn inward, startled watery eyes; no Mom in frame.

### 第 5 页

她低着头，坐到小凳子上。鞋就在脚边，她却迟迟没有动。

She sat on the little bench with her head down. Her shoes were beside her feet, but she did not move.

分镜：Medium low entryway view: An'an sits withdrawn on oak shoe bench, head bowed and eyes down toward floor, sad closed mouth, NO SMILE, plain white socks; unworn white red-trim sneakers beside her feet, red toy car under bench.

### 第 6 页

妈妈走近时，安安小声问：“妈妈，你是在凶我吗？”

When Mom came closer, An'an whispered, “Mom, are you being cross with me?”

分镜：Two-person intimate entryway shot: seated An'an looks up uncertainly; Mom bends closer with concerned expression, canvas bag on floor.

### 第 7 页

妈妈停住了。她蹲下来，看见安安紧紧攥着衣角。

Mom stopped. She crouched down and saw An'an gripping the edge of her hoodie.

分镜：Close two-person view at child eye level: Mom notices An'an's hands gripping teal hoodie hem, both faces readable.

### 第 8 页

“刚才我着急，声音太大，吓到你了。”妈妈轻声说，“对不起。”

“I was in a hurry, and my voice got too loud. It frightened you,” Mom said softly. “I'm sorry.”

分镜：Medium eye-level entryway: Mom crouches with relaxed open hands and apologetic face; An'an listens on bench, no forced embrace.

### 第 9 页

安安没有马上笑。她松开一点衣角：“我以为，你觉得我很烦。”

An'an did not smile right away. She loosened her grip a little. “I thought you found me annoying.”

分镜：Close An'an seated on bench, fingers gradually releasing hoodie hem, small tear and hesitant expression; Mom's listening profile visible.

### 第 10 页

“你不是很烦。”妈妈说，“我想提醒出门的时间，可是刚才没有好好说。”

“You aren't annoying,” Mom said. “I wanted to remind you about leaving, but I didn't say it kindly.”

分镜：Medium conversation: Mom listens and explains gently at An'an's height, round wall clock softly blurred behind them, no written markings.

### 第 11 页

妈妈重新说了一遍：“我们该穿鞋了。你的小车，还需要我帮哪一点？”

Mom tried again. “It's time to put our shoes on. Do you need any help with your car?”

分镜：Two-person low shot: Mom gestures gently toward white red-trim shoes and red toy car, An'an begins to respond.

### 第 12 页

“它总滑出来。”安安指了指凳子下面。原来，那里有一小块斜斜的地毯。

“It keeps rolling out,” An'an said, pointing beneath the bench. A small edge of the rug was tilted there.

分镜：Close floor view: An'an points to folded rug edge beneath red toy car; Mom's hand rests nearby, no extra people.

### 第 13 页

她们把地毯铺平，小车终于停稳了。安安这才伸手拿鞋。

They flattened the rug, and the car finally stayed put. Then An'an reached for her shoes.

分镜：Low medium entryway: Mom smooths rug while An'an picks up white red-trim sneaker; red toy car rests motionless beneath bench.

### 第 14 页

“你小声一点，我也能听见。”安安说。妈妈点点头：“我记住了。”

“I can hear you when you speak more quietly too,” An'an said. Mom nodded. “I'll remember.”

分镜：Warm medium shot: An'an seated putting on shoes speaks to crouching Mom, both relaxed but thoughtful, canvas bag beside doorway.

### 第 15 页

到了楼下，妈妈从远处叫她。安安抬起头，朝妈妈挥了挥手。

Downstairs, Mom called to her from farther away. An'an looked up and waved.

分镜：Wide safe apartment courtyard: Mom calls across a short open path; An'an stands nearby and waves, no road or cars moving.

### 第 16 页

妈妈走近，轻声说：“走这边。”安安牵住她的手，一起绕过了花坛。

Mom came closer and said softly, “This way.” An'an took her hand, and they walked around the flower bed.

分镜：Medium courtyard walking shot: Mom and An'an hold hands around low flower bed, calm expressions, cream canvas bag on Mom's shoulder.

### 第 17 页

后来，妈妈又有一次说急了。她停一下，换了轻一点的声音。

Later, Mom spoke too sharply once more. She paused and tried a gentler voice.

分镜：Home entryway another day: Mom visibly relaxes raised shoulders before speaking again; An'an looks toward her, red toy car parked under bench.

### 第 18 页

安安也慢慢学会了说：“刚才我有点怕。你可以再说一遍吗？”这一次，话没有缩回去。

An'an slowly learned to say, “That frightened me a little. Could you say it again?” This time, her words did not hide.

分镜：Warm close ending: An'an confidently speaks to attentive Mom at child eye level, soft afternoon light and oak bench behind them.

共读问题：
- 妈妈声音变大时，安安的身体有什么变化？
- 想请别人轻一点说话，你愿意怎样告诉他？

小活动：大人用两种温和的音量说同一句普通提醒，让孩子选择听着舒服的方式，并约好一个轻声提醒的手势。

## 我还没说，眼泪就来了

副标题：眼泪旁边，也可以有一句话

寓意：难过时可以哭；被提醒的一件事，可以等心情缓下来再一起处理。

亲子目标：允许孩子流泪和停顿，把具体行为与孩子的自我价值分开。

家长提醒：不把哭称作玻璃心、撒娇或故意逃避；不以停止哭泣为获得陪伴的条件。成人只谈具体事情，避免追问和人格评价。

### 第 1 页

安安画了一条蓝蓝的小河，又在岸边添上几朵花。她举起画纸，想给妈妈看。

An'an painted a blue river and added flowers along its bank. She lifted the paper to show Mom.

分镜：Wide oak art table: An'an proudly lifts painting of blue river and red flowers; Mom comes beside table; used blue-tipped brush lies on bare table.

### 第 2 页

妈妈看见桌上的画笔：“画笔用完，要先洗一洗，再放回笔筒。”

Mom noticed the brush on the table. “When we finish painting, we wash the brush before putting it away.”

分镜：Medium tabletop conversation: Mom gently indicates used blue-tipped brush and plain yellow brush cup; An'an holds river painting.

### 第 3 页

安安的嘴角慢慢往下掉。她把画纸放回桌上，低下了头。

An'an's smile slowly faded. She put the painting down and lowered her head.

分镜：Close portrait: An'an lowers gaze with trembling mouth; blue river painting lies on oak table, yellow brush cup nearby.

### 第 4 页

她还没想好怎么说，眼泪就先出来了。一滴，落在画纸空白的角上。

Before she knew what to say, the tears came first. One drop landed on a blank corner of the paper.

分镜：Close view: single tear falls onto blank corner of river painting beneath An'an's face, flowers and river remain intact.

### 第 5 页

妈妈把想继续说的话停了下来，轻轻把纸巾放在她伸手够得到的地方。

Mom stopped what she was about to say and placed a tissue within easy reach.

分镜：Medium art table: Mom quietly places tissue beside An'an, child remains tearful; yellow brush cup and river painting visible.

### 第 6 页

“眼泪来了。”妈妈说，“你可以先哭一会儿，我坐在这里。”

“The tears have come,” Mom said. “You can cry for a while. I'll sit here with you.”

分镜：Two-person seated side view: Mom sits calmly beside crying An'an without touching or demanding eye contact, warm indoor light.

### 第 7 页

安安用纸巾擦了擦脸。妈妈没有催她解释，画笔也暂时留在桌上。

An'an wiped her face. Mom did not hurry her to explain, and the brush stayed on the table for now.

分镜：Close hands: An'an holds tissue to cheek; Mom's relaxed hands rest on lap, used brush still on table beside painting.

### 第 8 页

过了一会儿，安安说：“你是不是觉得，我什么都做不好？”

After a while, An'an said, “Do you think I can't do anything right?”

分镜：Medium child-eye-level view: An'an speaks hesitantly to attentive seated Mom, tissue in hand, river painting between them.

### 第 9 页

妈妈摇摇头：“我说的是这支画笔需要洗，不是你这个人不好。”

Mom shook her head. “I meant this brush needs washing. I didn't mean there is anything wrong with you.”

分镜：Close two-person conversation: Mom gently points to used brush, not at child; An'an listens with moist eyes.

### 第 10 页

“可是，我还想让你看看小河。”安安指了指蓝色的弯弯。

“But I wanted you to look at my river too,” An'an said, pointing to its blue bends.

分镜：Overhead tabletop: An'an's finger indicates winding blue river painting, Mom's hand rests beside blank corner, no words on paper.

### 第 11 页

妈妈把画放在眼前：“这里像绕过了一块小石头。你给它安排了什么地方？”

Mom brought the painting closer. “It looks as if the river goes around a little stone here. Where is it flowing?”

分镜：Medium art table: Mom studies river painting closely with curious face; An'an points toward one bend, used brush remains aside.

### 第 12 页

安安说起了小河、花和一条没画出来的小鱼。说着说着，她的呼吸慢了下来。

An'an told her about the river, flowers, and a fish she had not painted yet. As she spoke, her breathing grew calmer.

分镜：Close An'an telling painting story, cheeks still damp but shoulders relaxed; Mom listens, no imaginary live fish in frame.

### 第 13 页

“画笔还要洗。”妈妈轻声提醒。“我知道。”安安说，“你陪我一起去吗？”

“The brush still needs washing,” Mom reminded her softly. “I know,” An'an said. “Will you come with me?”

分镜：Medium table: An'an picks up used blue-tipped brush and looks toward Mom for help; Mom nods gently.

### 第 14 页

她们走到水池边。安安把笔尖放进水里，蓝色慢慢散开了。

They went to the sink. An'an dipped the brush into the water, and the blue color slowly spread.

分镜：Close safe sink: An'an rinses blue-tipped brush in shallow basin while Mom stands beside her, low water flow, no drinking.

### 第 15 页

洗干净的画笔回到笔筒里。画纸放在窗边，空白角上的水痕也快干了。

The clean brush went back into its cup. The painting rested by the window, and the water mark on its blank corner was nearly dry.

分镜：Still life close view: clean brush in plain yellow cup beside blue-river painting by window, faint damp mark only on blank corner; no people.

### 第 16 页

第二天，爸爸提醒桌边的杯子要挪远一点。安安又觉得鼻子有点酸。

The next day, Dad reminded her to move a cup away from the edge. An'an felt the tears coming again.

分镜：Medium breakfast table: Dad gently gestures toward small unmarked cup near edge; An'an looks emotional, safe seated setting.

### 第 17 页

这一次，她说：“我有点难过，等我一下。”爸爸点点头，先把杯子放稳。

This time, she said, “I'm feeling upset. Give me a moment.” Dad nodded and made sure the cup was safe.

分镜：Two-person close view: An'an quietly expresses feelings while Dad moves cup inward, no spilled liquid, attentive expression.

### 第 18 页

眼泪有时候还会先来。安安发现，眼泪旁边，也能慢慢长出一句自己的话。

Sometimes the tears still came first. An'an discovered that her own words could slowly appear beside them.

分镜：Warm ending portrait: An'an holds dry river painting and speaks to Mom, slight watery eyes and small steady smile, yellow brush cup behind.

共读问题：
- 妈妈提醒画笔时，安安心里听成了什么？
- 眼泪来的时候，你希望身边的人怎样陪你？

小活动：用玩偶练习一次温和提醒，并尝试“我有点难过”“等我一下”或指一指；孩子愿意时再共同完成一件小事。

## 那句话卡在嘴巴里

副标题：从指一指开始，也是在表达

寓意：犯错后说不出来时，可以先求助，再慢慢说明和补救。

亲子目标：帮助孩子在犯错后恢复表达，体验说明经过和共同补救的安全感。

家长提醒：不把僵住、沉默或说不清认作撒谎、无所谓或故意对抗；不用连环追问逼出坦白。允许指认、点头和成人陪同表达。

### 第 1 页

乐乐搭了一座蓝色积木桥。桥下留着一条小路，安安想让木头小船从那里经过。

Lele built a blue block bridge with a passage beneath it. An'an wanted her little wooden boat to travel through.

分镜：Wide playroom: Lele beside intact blue block bridge on beige mat; An'an holds small plain wooden boat near bridge passage.

### 第 2 页

小船有点宽。安安伸手挪旁边的一块积木，想让它通过。

The boat was a little wide. An'an reached to move one block so it could fit through.

分镜：Close play mat: An'an's hand reaches toward one blue support block, wooden boat rests beside intact bridge; Lele watches.

### 第 3 页

哗啦一下，桥的一边倒了。蓝色积木滚到她的鞋尖前。

With a clatter, one side of the bridge fell. A blue block rolled to her shoe.

分镜：Low floor close view: one side of blue block bridge collapsed, loose blue blocks and wooden boat on beige mat beside An'an's white red-trim shoe.

### 第 4 页

乐乐看着断开的桥，愣住了：“我刚搭好……”

Lele stared at the broken bridge. “I'd just finished it...”

分镜：Medium Lele disappointed beside partly collapsed blue bridge, hands paused on knees; An'an stands at edge of frame.

### 第 5 页

安安想说“是我碰倒的”，嘴巴却像被什么堵住。她站在那里，手垂着。

An'an wanted to say, “I knocked it down,” but her mouth felt blocked. She stood there with her hands hanging still.

分镜：Close portrait: An'an stands frozen with tense mouth and lowered hands, blue blocks visible near shoes; only An'an visible.

### 第 6 页

妈妈走来，看见地上的积木，也看见两个孩子都没有动。

Mom came over. She saw the blocks on the floor and the two children standing still.

分镜：Wide playroom: Mom approaches An'an and seated Lele beside partly collapsed blue bridge on beige mat, wooden boat nearby.

### 第 7 页

她先把走路的地方空出来，再蹲到安安旁边：“你想让我陪着吗？”

She cleared a safe place to walk, then crouched beside An'an. “Would you like me to stay with you?”

分镜：Medium child-eye-level shot: Mom crouches beside frozen An'an after moving loose blocks off walkway; Lele stays beside bridge.

### 第 8 页

安安点了一下头。妈妈没有接着问，只把手轻轻放在自己的膝盖上。

An'an nodded once. Mom did not ask another question. She simply rested her hands on her own knees.

分镜：Close two-person view: An'an gives tiny nod; crouching Mom waits with hands on own knees, no gripping child.

### 第 9 页

“如果现在说不出，可以指给我看。”妈妈说。安安指了指那块蓝色积木。

“If words won't come yet, you can point,” Mom said. An'an pointed at the blue block.

分镜：Close floor-level shot: An'an points to displaced blue support block beside partly collapsed bridge; Mom follows her gesture.

### 第 10 页

妈妈问：“你刚才动了这块？”安安又点点头。卡住的话，松开了一点。

Mom asked, “Did you move this one?” An'an nodded again. The stuck words loosened a little.

分镜：Medium quiet conversation: Mom gently asks An'an while indicating support block; child nods, Lele visible calmly nearby.

### 第 11 页

“我想让小船过去。”安安终于说，“然后，桥就倒了。”

“I wanted the boat to go through,” An'an finally said. “Then the bridge fell.”

分镜：Close An'an speaking hesitantly while holding plain wooden boat; Mom listens, collapsed bridge softly visible behind.

### 第 12 页

妈妈转向乐乐：“安安想把经过告诉你。我们一起听完，好吗？”

Mom turned to Lele. “An'an wants to tell you what happened. Shall we hear her out together?”

分镜：Three-person medium playroom: Mom gently addresses seated Lele; An'an stands beside her holding wooden boat, scattered blue blocks on mat.

### 第 13 页

安安看着乐乐：“我没有先问你，就挪了积木。对不起。”

An'an looked at Lele. “I moved a block without asking you first. I'm sorry.”

分镜：Two-child close conversation: An'an apologizes sincerely to disappointed Lele, Mom quietly behind at a respectful distance.

### 第 14 页

乐乐说：“我还很难过。我想先坐一会儿。”安安把小船放下，没有催他。

Lele said, “I'm still upset. I want to sit for a while.” An'an put down the boat and did not hurry him.

分镜：Medium play mat: Lele sits with upset expression; An'an places wooden boat aside and gives him space, no forced handshake.

### 第 15 页

过了一会儿，乐乐指着桥墩：“你可以帮我找这些蓝色的。”

After a while, Lele pointed at the supports. “You can help me find these blue ones.”

分镜：Close Lele points toward blue support blocks on beige mat; An'an looks attentive, partly collapsed bridge remains.

### 第 16 页

安安一块一块找出来。她先问：“这块放这里，对吗？”

An'an found the blocks one by one. Before placing one, she asked, “Does this one go here?”

分镜：Overhead play mat: An'an offers blue block to Lele before placing it, reconstruction in progress, wooden boat parked aside.

### 第 17 页

桥慢慢站起来了。这一次，他们一起给小船留了一条宽一点的路。

The bridge slowly stood again. This time, they left a wider passage for the boat together.

分镜：Medium two-child view: Lele and An'an rebuild stable blue block bridge with visibly wider opening, wooden boat beside opening.

### 第 18 页

妈妈说：“愿意说出发生的事，让我们知道可以从哪里帮忙。”

Mom said, “Telling us what happened helps us see where we can help.”

分镜：Warm three-person medium shot: Mom talks beside children and rebuilt blue bridge, both children listen, no triumphant poses.

### 第 19 页

安安摸了摸小船：“下次卡住的时候，我可以先说，陪我一下。”

An'an touched the boat. “Next time my words get stuck, I can start with, 'Stay with me for a moment.'”

分镜：Close An'an holding wooden boat thoughtfully beside rebuilt blue bridge, Lele nearby with relaxed face.

### 第 20 页

小船终于过了桥。安安知道，有些话可以慢一点；该做的补救，也能一步一步来。

The boat finally passed beneath the bridge. An'an knew words could take time, and making things right could happen one step at a time.

分镜：Low warm ending: An'an guides wooden boat beneath wide blue bridge while Lele watches, Mom sits nearby, beige play mat.

共读问题：
- 积木桥倒下后，安安为什么站着不动？
- 一时说不出来时，你可以怎样让别人知道需要帮助？

小活动：用积木演一个小意外，让孩子任选指一指、点头、画出来或请成人陪着说，再一起商量一个补救动作。

## 我只想吃这一种

副标题：喜欢的味道，也可以慢慢选

寓意：可以有喜欢的口味，也可以在清楚的安排里选择和收尾。

亲子目标：尊重口味偏好，帮助孩子参与零食安排并应对最喜欢的食物暂时吃完。

家长提醒：不把挑口味说成贪心或不懂珍惜；不强迫尝新，不以正餐或零食惩罚、奖励行为。根据孩子需要灵活安排，成人确认食物安全。

### 第 1 页

下午点心时间，妈妈摆出两种小饼干：紫薯的，和原味的。安安先看见了紫色那一盘。

At snack time, Mom put out sweet-potato crackers and plain ones. An'an noticed the purple plate first.

分镜：Wide kitchen oak table: Mom sets purple sweet-potato crackers on small white plate and beige plain crackers on another; An'an sits nearby.

### 第 2 页

“我只想吃这一种！”她把小碗推到紫薯饼干旁边。

“I only want these!” she said, pushing her bowl beside the sweet-potato crackers.

分镜：Close tabletop: An'an slides plain teal bowl beside plate of purple crackers, beige crackers remain on separate white plate.

### 第 3 页

妈妈问：“是喜欢它的味道，还是喜欢脆脆的声音？”

Mom asked, “Do you like their taste, or the crunchy sound?”

分镜：Medium conversation: Mom leans beside seated An'an, child looks at purple crackers and teal bowl, both calm.

### 第 4 页

“都有。”安安咬了一小口，“还甜甜的。”

“Both,” An'an said, taking a small bite. “And they're sweet.”

分镜：Close An'an seated safely taking small bite of purple cracker, teal bowl on table, no running or full mouth.

### 第 5 页

妈妈点点头：“可以选你喜欢的。我们先盛一小碗，看看吃得怎样。”

Mom nodded. “You can choose what you like. Let's start with a small bowl and see how you feel.”

分镜：Overhead table: Mom and An'an put small serving of purple crackers into teal bowl, remaining crackers on white plate, no exact number.

### 第 6 页

安安吃着吃着，碗空了。盘子里的紫薯饼干，也没有了。

An'an ate until the bowl was empty. The sweet-potato plate was empty too.

分镜：Close tabletop: empty teal bowl and empty white purple-cracker plate; beige plain crackers still on separate plate, An'an's hands nearby.

### 第 7 页

### 第 18 页

后来，安安和妈妈在平静的时候练了一句：“停一下，我很生气。”

Later, when they were calm, An'an and Mom practiced saying, “Stop a moment. I'm angry.”

分镜：Home sofa medium shot: calm An'an and Mom use two simple wooden dolls to rehearse speaking, no conflict or violence.

### 第 19 页

她们还选了一个求助手势：把手掌举起来，让妈妈看见。

They chose a signal for asking for help too: holding up a palm where Mom could see it.

分镜：Close calm practice: An'an holds one open palm upward to signal help, Mom acknowledges, wooden dolls on sofa cushion.

### 第 20 页

生气有时还会来。安安的小手慢慢记住了：先停一下，再找一句能说出来的话。

Anger would still visit sometimes. An'an's little hands slowly learned to pause first and find words she could say.

分镜：Warm ending playroom: An'an holds open palm and calmly speaks beside intact red-roof block house, Mom attentive, Xiaoyu at respectful distance.

共读问题：
- 安安生气时，小手和身体发生了什么变化？
- 别人碰倒作品时，你想怎样告诉他，又需要谁帮忙？

小活动：在平静时用玩偶练习“停一下，我很生气”“请先问我”，一起选一个安全的退开位置或求助手势；不用制造真实冲突来练习。

## 这一集，我还想看

副标题：结束之后，我们一起去做下一件事

寓意：停下屏幕需要清楚的安排和陪伴，大人也一起遵守自己的约定。

亲子目标：认识自动连播与难以收尾的情境，用预告、具体结束点和共同活动帮助过渡。

家长提醒：不把停不下说成意志差，不用羞辱、突然夺走或额外奖励来结束；安排要适龄且可落实。成人负责设置设备，不能把全部自控责任交给孩子。

### 第 1 页

星期六下午，安安坐在沙发上看动画。屏幕里的彩色小圆点，正滚过一座小桥。

On Saturday afternoon, An'an watched an animation on the sofa. Colorful little dots rolled over a tiny bridge on the screen.

分镜：Wide living room: seated An'an watches gray-cased tablet showing abstract colored circles and simple bridge, Mom sits nearby with phone, no text.

### 第 2 页

她和妈妈约好，看完这一集，就一起做纸盒停车场。

She and Mom had agreed to make a cardboard parking lot together after this episode.

分镜：Medium sofa: Mom and An'an beside gray tablet, plain cardboard boxes and small red toy car ready on low oak table.

### 第 3 页

片尾音乐响起来了。安安坐直了一点，准备叫妈妈。

The ending music began. An'an sat up a little, ready to call Mom.

分镜：Close An'an beside tablet displaying simple ending animation of colored circles, Mom holding phone softly blurred behind.

### 第 4 页

可是屏幕闪了一下，下一集自己开始了。新的小圆点又跑出来。

But the screen flickered, and the next episode started by itself. New colorful dots appeared.

分镜：Close tablet in gray case: fresh abstract circle animation begins, An'an's face reflects renewed attention, no titles or playback text.

### 第 5 页

“我想把这个也看完。”安安说。妈妈抬起头：“我们刚才约好结束了。”

“I want to finish this one too,” An'an said. Mom looked up. “We agreed to stop after the last one.”

分镜：Medium sofa conversation: An'an still gazes at tablet, Mom looks up from phone, cardboard project on table untouched.

### 第 6 页

安安抱紧平板：“可它已经开始了。”她觉得，好像有一根线把眼睛拉住。

An'an held the tablet tightly. “But it's already started.” It felt as if a little string were pulling her eyes toward it.

分镜：Close An'an holding gray tablet with reluctant expression, abstract colorful display; no literal string or fantasy imagery.

### 第 7 页

妈妈先停住播放：“下一集自己开始，确实更难收尾。我来把这个设置关掉。”

Mom paused playback. “When the next episode starts by itself, stopping is harder. I'll turn that setting off.”

分镜：Close Mom uses tablet carefully while An'an watches; screen dim with simple unlabeled controls, no visible writing.

### 第 8 页

妈妈把平板放在桌上，坐回安安旁边。安安的眉毛还皱着。

Mom put the tablet on the table and sat beside An'an again. An'an was still frowning.

分镜：Medium sofa: gray tablet lies paused on oak table beside cardboard boxes; Mom stays beside disappointed An'an.

### 第 9 页

“我还想知道后面发生什么。”安安说。妈妈点头：“我们记住，下次从这里开始。”

“I still want to know what happens next,” An'an said. Mom nodded. “We'll remember where to start next time.”

分镜：Two-person close conversation: Mom acknowledges An'an's curiosity, child points toward paused tablet, no bargain or new treat.

### 第 10 页

她们画了一个小桥的图案，夹在纸盒旁边。喜欢的故事，有了等下次的地方。

They drew a tiny bridge and placed the picture beside the boxes. The story she liked had a place to wait for next time.

分镜：Overhead low table: plain paper with simple bridge drawing beside gray tablet and cardboard boxes, child's pencil and Mom's hand, no text.

### 第 11 页

妈妈拿起纸盒，安安没有马上跟过去：“等我坐一会儿。”

Mom picked up a box. An'an did not join in immediately. “Let me sit for a little while.”

分镜：Wide living room: Mom sits on rug with plain cardboard box; An'an remains on sofa taking a pause, tablet dark on table.

### 第 12 页

过了一会儿，她滑下沙发：“停车场的门，要开在哪里？”

After a while, she slid down from the sofa. “Where should the parking-lot entrance go?”

分镜：Medium low view: An'an joins Mom beside cardboard boxes and points at possible entrance, adult handles any cutting out of frame.

### 第 13 页

妈妈刚要回答，手机响了。她看了一眼，又放到柜子上：“这会儿是我们的纸盒时间。”

Just as Mom was about to answer, her phone chimed. She glanced at it, then put it on the cupboard. “This is our cardboard time.”

分镜：Medium living room: Mom places phone high on pale green cabinet, An'an waits beside cardboard boxes, tablet remains dark.

### 第 14 页

安安扶住纸盒，妈妈帮忙开好了门。小红车慢慢开进了停车场。

An'an steadied the box, and Mom helped make the entrance. The little red car slowly drove into its parking lot.

分镜：Close cardboard project: completed safe opening in plain box, An'an guides small red toy car through it, Mom supports box, no blades visible.

### 第 15 页

下一次看动画前，她们先选好结束的位置，还把下一件事摆在旁边。

Before the next viewing, they chose a clear stopping point and set out the next activity nearby.

分镜：Medium living room another day: Mom and An'an prepare gray tablet beside simple red-sand hourglass and cardboard project, no numbers or readable interface.

### 第 16 页

片尾音乐响了，安安还是想再看。妈妈轻声提醒，陪她把平板收好。

When the ending music played, An'an still wanted more. Mom gently reminded her and helped put the tablet away.

分镜：Two-person medium shot: Mom helps willing-but-disappointed An'an place gray tablet into plain storage basket, no grabbing.

### 第 17 页

安安说：“我还想看，不过明天可以接着。”她摸摸纸盒里的小车，先把它开出来。

An'an said, “I still want to watch, but I can continue tomorrow.” She touched the car in the box and drove it out.

分镜：Close An'an rolls small red toy car out of cardboard parking lot, tablet stored on shelf, thoughtful rather than forced cheerful expression.

### 第 18 页

屏幕停下了，喜欢还在。接下来的时间，安安和妈妈一起慢慢走进去。

The screen had stopped, and her interest remained. An'an and Mom moved into the next part of their day together.

分镜：Warm ending wide living room: Mom and An'an play with cardboard parking lot on rug, phone on cabinet and tablet stored away, no screens lit.

共读问题：
- 下一集自己开始时，安安为什么更难停下？
- 结束屏幕之后，你希望和家人接着做什么？

小活动：成人关闭自动连播，与孩子提前约好一个容易辨认的结束点和下一件小活动；成人也为自己的手机安排结束提醒。

## 这次真的不是我

副标题：请先听我把经过说完

寓意：被误会时可以求助和说明；大人也需要核对事实，为误会道歉。

亲子目标：给被误会的孩子表达空间，成人示范停止猜测、听取经过与承担误会的责任。

家长提醒：不要求孩子靠声音大、立即说清或拿出证据才值得被信任；不以过去犯错推定这次也是他。需要澄清时由成人协助，不让孩子独自对抗。

### 第 1 页

安安和小宇一起画画。桌上放着一小杯橙色颜料，妈妈在旁边整理画纸。

An'an and Xiaoyu painted together. A small cup of orange paint sat on the table while Mom arranged paper nearby.

分镜：Wide oak art table: An'an and Xiaoyu paint with small plain orange-paint cup between them; Mom arranges blank paper beside table.

### 第 2 页

安安举起自己的画，转过身，想放到窗边晾干。

An'an lifted her painting and turned toward the window to let it dry.

分镜：Medium art corner: An'an carries painting with both hands away from table toward window; Xiaoyu remains seated beside orange paint cup.

### 第 3 页

小宇去拿蓝色画笔，袖子碰到了杯子。橙色颜料沿桌面流出来。

Xiaoyu reached for a blue brush, and his sleeve caught the cup. Orange paint spread across the table.

分镜：Close tabletop event: Xiaoyu's green sleeve tips plain orange paint cup while reaching for blue brush, An'an visible turned away holding painting.

### 第 4 页

妈妈听见声音，回头看见安安站在桌旁：“安安，你怎么把颜料碰倒了？”

Mom heard the sound and turned. Seeing An'an beside the table, she said, “An'an, how did you knock the paint over?”

分镜：Medium three-person art table: Mom turns and mistakenly addresses startled An'an holding painting, Xiaoyu sits beside tipped cup and orange spill.

### 第 5 页

“不是……”安安的脸一下红了。她急着解释，话却挤在一起。

“I didn't...” An'an's face flushed. She hurried to explain, but her words crowded together.

分镜：Close An'an flushed and distressed holding dry painting with both hands, mouth hesitates, orange spill blurred behind.

### 第 6 页

“我都转过去了，小宇的……”她越急，越说不清，眼泪也涌了上来。

“I'd already turned around, and Xiaoyu's...” The more she hurried, the harder it was to speak. Tears rose too.

分镜：Close An'an with watery eyes and tense shoulders, painting held safely, Mom's concerned profile nearby.

### 第 7 页

妈妈停了一下。她把颜料杯放稳，铺上抹布，再蹲到安安面前。

Mom paused. She steadied the cup, laid a cloth over the spill, and crouched in front of An'an.

分镜：Medium art corner: Mom has set paint cup upright and cloth over orange spill, crouches before upset An'an; Xiaoyu seated nearby.

### 第 8 页

“我刚才还没问经过，就先说是你。”妈妈说，“我们慢一点，你愿意说时，我听着。”

“I said it was you before asking what happened,” Mom said. “Let's slow down. I'll listen when you're ready.”

分镜：Intimate two-person eye-level shot: Mom apologetically listens to An'an with open hands, no demands for eye contact.

### 第 9 页

安安把画放到旁边，擦擦眼睛：“这次真的不是我。”

An'an set her painting aside and wiped her eyes. “It really wasn't me this time.”

分镜：Medium child at window ledge: An'an lays painting flat and wipes cheek with tissue, Mom listens beside her.

### 第 10 页

“我拿着画，已经转过去了。”她用手指了指自己刚才站的地方。

“I was holding my painting and had already turned around.” She pointed to where she had stood.

分镜：Wide art room: An'an points from window toward her earlier spot by table, Mom follows gesture, tipped-paint area covered by cloth.

### 第 11 页

小宇低头看着袖口：“是我的袖子碰到的。我刚才也吓了一跳。”

Xiaoyu looked at his cuff. “My sleeve caught it. I was startled too.”

分镜：Close Xiaoyu looks at small orange stain on green sleeve cuff and speaks honestly; upright orange paint cup nearby, no shaming.

### 第 12 页

妈妈认真听完：“谢谢你们说明。安安，我误会你了，对不起。”

Mom heard them out. “Thank you for explaining. An'an, I misunderstood you. I'm sorry.”

分镜：Three-person medium shot: Mom sincerely apologizes to An'an; Xiaoyu nearby with stained cuff, cloth covers spill.

### 第 13 页

安安说：“你刚才一说，我就觉得怎么解释都没用了。”妈妈轻轻点头。

An'an said, “When you said it was me, I felt as if explaining wouldn't help.” Mom nodded gently.

分镜：Close two-person conversation: An'an shares hurt with still-damp eyes, Mom listens thoughtfully without defending herself.

### 第 14 页

“以后我先问发生了什么。”妈妈说，“你也可以提醒我，先听你讲完。”

“Next time, I'll ask what happened first,” Mom said. “You can remind me to hear you out too.”

分镜：Medium art corner: Mom and An'an talk beside drying painting, relaxed attentive posture, no pointing accusation.

### 第 15 页

小宇和妈妈一起擦桌子。安安先坐了一会儿，再决定把自己的画纸收好。

Xiaoyu and Mom wiped the table. An'an sat for a while, then chose to put away her own paper.

分镜：Wide art room: Mom and Xiaoyu wipe orange spill with cloth; An'an separately sits beside dry papers, not forced to clean others' mistake.

### 第 16 页

过了几天，地上的积木倒了。妈妈先问：“刚才这里发生了什么？”

A few days later, blocks fell on the floor. Mom first asked, “What happened here?”

分镜：Medium playroom: scattered wooden blocks on mat, Mom calmly asks An'an and Xiaoyu from equal eye level, no accusing gestures.

### 第 17 页

安安说：“是我拿盒子时碰到的。我来收。”她发现，妈妈真的在等她把话说完。

An'an said, “I knocked them over while taking the box. I'll tidy them.” She saw that Mom really waited for her to finish.

分镜：Close An'an honestly explains beside plain storage box and fallen blocks; Mom waits attentively, Xiaoyu nearby.

### 第 18 页

那句“请先听我说”留在安安心里。说出经过，可以让事情更清楚，也让委屈有地方被听见。

“Please hear me out” stayed with An'an. Explaining could make things clearer and give her hurt feelings a place to be heard.

分镜：Warm ending: An'an speaks calmly to attentive Mom beside neatly stored blocks, relaxed hands and thoughtful expression, no text.

共读问题：
- 妈妈一开始知道了什么，又猜了什么？
- 被误会时，你希望大人先做哪一件事？

小活动：用玩偶演一次物品倒下的小意外，分别练习“请先听我说”“我还不知道，先问问经过”；成人也练习具体道歉。

## 那声你好，藏在背后

副标题：慢一点，也可以找到自己的招呼

寓意：和不熟悉的人打招呼，可以慢慢来，选择让自己舒服的方式。

亲子目标：尊重孩子面对不熟悉的人时的节奏，在可靠成人陪伴下尝试自己的问候方式。

家长提醒：不以没礼貌、胆小评价孩子，不强迫说话、身体接触或接受陌生人的礼物；问候不代表同意跟随，安全边界仍由成人负责。

### 第 1 页

爸爸牵着安安下楼，遇见了他熟悉的邻居阿姨。阿姨正在给花浇水。

Dad took An'an downstairs and met a neighbor he knew. She was watering her flowers.

分镜：Wide safe apartment courtyard: Dad holds An'an's hand near familiar neighbor Aunt watering potted flowers with small plain can.

### 第 2 页

“早上好呀。”阿姨笑着说。安安一下躲到爸爸身后，只露出半只鞋。

“Good morning,” the neighbor said with a smile. An'an slipped behind Dad, leaving only part of one shoe showing.

分镜：Medium courtyard: friendly Aunt greets from respectful distance; An'an hides behind Dad with one white red-trim sneaker visible.

### 第 3 页

那声“你好”已经到了嘴边，却又缩了回去。她把爸爸的手攥得更紧。

“Hello” was almost at An'an's lips, then retreated. She held Dad's hand more tightly.

分镜：Close An'an partly hidden behind Dad's navy sweater, hand gripping his gently, shy uncertain eyes, no extra people.

### 第 4 页

爸爸没有把她拉出来。他说：“安安想先在我旁边待一会儿。”

Dad did not pull her forward. “An'an would like to stay beside me for a little while,” he said.

分镜：Medium Dad calmly speaks to Aunt while An'an stays behind his leg, no pulling or pushing child forward.

### 第 5 页

阿姨点点头，继续浇花。水落在叶子上，亮晶晶的。

The neighbor nodded and went on watering. Drops sparkled on the leaves.

分镜：Close Aunt gently waters green potted leaves, sparkling water droplets, Dad and An'an at a respectful distance softly blurred.

### 第 6 页

回家路上，爸爸问：“刚才你是有点紧张，还是还不想说话？”

On the way home, Dad asked, “Were you feeling nervous, or did you simply not want to speak yet?”

分镜：Two-person medium apartment path: Dad and An'an walk holding hands, Dad asks gently, no Aunt in frame.

### 第 7 页

安安说：“她看着我，我就忘了怎么说。”爸爸点头：“原来是这样。”

An'an said, “When she looked at me, I forgot what to say.” Dad nodded. “I see.”

分镜：Close An'an quietly explains to Dad while walking, Dad listens without teasing, safe indoor hallway.

### 第 8 页

“你可以先点头，或者挥挥手。”爸爸说，“也可以今天先不试。”

“You could start with a nod or a wave,” Dad said. “And you can choose not to try today.”

分镜：Home sofa medium conversation: Dad demonstrates small gentle wave; An'an considers, no forced practice.

### 第 9 页

安安举起手，试着晃了一下：“这样，也算打招呼吗？”

An'an lifted her hand and gave a tiny wave. “Does this count as saying hello too?”

分镜：Close calm home scene: An'an gives tentative small wave, Dad smiles attentively, natural fingers.

### 第 10 页

“当然。”爸爸说。他们没有安排比赛，也没有要求声音一定要大。

“Of course,” Dad said. They made no contest of it and did not require a loud voice.

分镜：Medium home sofa: Dad and An'an relax after tiny wave practice, no chart, prizes or audience.

### 第 11 页

几天后，他们又遇见阿姨。这一次，安安站在爸爸旁边，轻轻挥了挥手。

A few days later, they met the neighbor again. This time, An'an stood beside Dad and gave a little wave.

分镜：Wide courtyard: An'an stands beside Dad and gives small wave to familiar Aunt near potted flowers, comfortable distance.

### 第 12 页

阿姨也挥挥手：“我看见啦。”说完，她又去照顾窗边的小花。

The neighbor waved back. “I saw that.” Then she returned to the flowers by her window.

分镜：Medium Aunt returns small wave without approaching or touching child; An'an and Dad remain beside courtyard path.

### 第 13 页

后来的一天，安安看见花盆里开了一朵小黄花。她停下来，多看了一会儿。

One day later, An'an noticed a little yellow flower in the pot. She stopped to look at it a while longer.

分镜：Close An'an observes small yellow flower in green potted plant, Dad beside her, Aunt farther away tending leaves.

### 第 14 页

“这是什么花？”她小声问。阿姨听见了，蹲下来，隔着花盆告诉她。

“What flower is that?” she asked softly. The neighbor heard and crouched on the other side of the pot to answer.

分镜：Medium courtyard: Aunt crouches across flower pot at respectful distance and answers softly; An'an beside Dad asks about yellow bloom.

### 第 15 页

走的时候，安安轻轻说：“阿姨，再见。”声音不大，却是她自己想说的。

When they left, An'an said softly, “Goodbye.” Her voice was quiet, and the words were her own choice.

分镜：Three-person courtyard farewell: An'an gives voluntary soft goodbye and wave beside Dad; Aunt smiles from flower pots.

### 第 16 页

那声你好有时还会藏在背后。有爸爸陪着，安安可以慢慢找到自己的方式。

Sometimes hello would still hide behind her. With Dad beside her, An'an could find her own way in her own time.

分镜：Warm ending safe courtyard: An'an walks beside Dad with relaxed hand in his, looks back with small wave, Aunt distant near flowers.

共读问题：
- 安安躲到爸爸身后时，心里可能有什么感觉？
- 除了说你好，还可以怎样向熟悉的人打招呼？

小活动：和孩子选择点头、挥手或一句问候，在家自愿用玩偶练习；外出由孩子决定是否尝试，不把打招呼变成表演考核。
