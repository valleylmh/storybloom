# 日常系列第 52 本逐页文稿与分镜（大班里的三位老师）

用户于 2026-11-03 确认：三位老师分别为班主任/主任林老师、副班/副主任张老师、生活老师江老师。

## 大班里的三位老师

副标题：不同的神奇本领，一样的温暖守护

寓意：分工合作让大班井井有条；遇到不同的事情找对帮手，我们也能成为老师的小帮手。

亲子目标：帮助孩子建立对幼儿园大班教师团队分工的社会认知，能在遇到生活、学习或运动问题时准确求助，体会分工合作的美好与集体生活的安全感。

家长提醒：避免用“哪个老师最凶/哪个老师最好”给老师贴标签；鼓励孩子观察不同老师的特长与职责，遇到问题学会向对应的老师清晰表达。

### 第 1 页

清晨的阳光洒在大班教室门口，门口的值日牌边，站着三位笑眯眯的老师。“早上好，安安！”三声清脆好听的问候，让新的一天暖洋洋地开始了。

Morning sunlight warmed the doorway of the senior classroom, where three smiling teachers stood by the duty chart. “Good morning, An'an!” Three cheerful greetings welcomed the brand-new day.

分镜：Bright morning view of senior kindergarten doorway: three teachers stand warmly together by wooden shelf; Teacher Lin in sage-green cardigan, Teacher Zhang in bright yellow top with high ponytail, Teacher Jiang in soft lilac cardigan; An'an in teal hoodie approaches smiling.

### 第 2 页

刚走进门，江老师就弯下腰，用温暖干燥的手心轻轻贴了贴安安的额头：“额头温温的，小脸红润润，今天也是健康的大班小朋友！”

Stepping inside, Teacher Jiang leaned down and gently touched An'an's forehead with warm, dry hands. “Your forehead is cool and your cheeks are rosy. A healthy day for our big kid!”

分镜：Warm close-up eye-level two-shot: Teacher Jiang with curly short hair and soft lilac cardigan gently checks An'an's forehead; An'an looks up with trusting bright eyes.

### 第 3 页

林老师在黑板前微笑着招招手：“安安，今天的晨间图书角由你负责，我们一起给故事书排好队吧。”她把金色小星红袖标端正地套在安安手臂上。

At the blackboard, Teacher Lin waved with a gentle smile. “An'an, you are on morning reading corner duty today. Let's line up the storybooks together.” She slipped the red star armband onto An'an's arm.

分镜：Classroom morning shot: Teacher Lin in sage-green cardigan fastens the red star armband onto An'an's teal sleeve; wooden blackboard with blank colorful duty slots in background.

### 第 4 页

“走，晨间活动先跟张老师去伸展筋骨！”张老师一身活力满满的鹅黄色上衣，高高地扎着马尾辫，轻快地拍了拍手，带着大家走向地垫区做动物操。

“Time to stretch before morning activities!” called Teacher Zhang in her sunny yellow sweater, her high ponytail swinging as she clapped briskly to lead everyone in animal stretches on the mats.

分镜：Active medium shot: Teacher Zhang with a lively high ponytail in bright yellow top leads stretching on colorful play mats; children raise arms eagerly.

### 第 5 页

上午的故事时间是林老师的大本领。她抱着一本厚厚的绘本，坐在大家围成的小圆圈中央，轻轻一翻页，教室里立刻安静得能听见呼吸声。

Morning storytime was Teacher Lin's special craft. Holding a thick picture book, she sat in the middle of the carpet circle; with one gentle turn of the page, the whole room grew quietly spellbound.

分镜：Cozy circle time: Teacher Lin in sage-green cardigan holds an open blank-cover picture book; An'an, Tiantian and Xiaoyu sit cross-legged on circular carpet listening with rapt faces.

### 第 6 页

安安听得入神，忍不住举起小手提问：“老师，小候鸟飞过大海，会觉得累吗？”林老师停下来，温柔地注视着她：“会呀，所以它们会排成整齐的人字形，轮流挡风休息呢。”

Listening closely, An'an raised her hand. “Teacher, when little birds fly over the wide sea, do their wings get tired?” Teacher Lin paused and looked warmly into her eyes. “They do, which is why they fly together in a neat V-line, taking turns breaking the wind.”

分镜：Medium two-person view: An'an raising one straight hand with curious face; Teacher Lin kneeling beside her chair, nodding attentively at eye level.

### 第 7 页

下一节是大型积木建构课，这可是张老师最拿手的！她从大木箱里抱出一捆长横梁：“今天我们要搭一座能通过两辆小车的超级双层大桥！”

Next came giant block building, Teacher Zhang's favorite realm! She lifted a bundle of long wooden beams from a crate. “Today we'll build a double-decker bridge wide enough for two toy cars!”

分镜：Spacious building zone: Teacher Zhang in yellow sweater kneels by wooden crates holding long timber planks; An'an and Xiaoyu gather around with excited smiles.

### 第 8 页

可是桥墩搭到第三层时，积木晃了晃，“哗啦”一声塌倒了一片。天天沮丧地坐在地上，安安也缩回了小手。

But when the third layer went up, the tower wobbled, and with a soft clatter, several blocks tumbled down. Tiantian slumped onto the floor, and An'an pulled her hands back.

分镜：Floor level shot: a half-toppled wooden block bridge on the mat; Tiantian in blue sweatshirt sits dejectedly, An'an looking worried with open hands.

### 第 9 页

张老师大步走过来，不仅没批评，还笑着拍了拍木块：“倒了太正常啦！桥墩底座要三个木块并排才站得稳，我们一起当稳稳的工程师！”在她的带领下，大桥立得又高又坚实。

Teacher Zhang stepped over with a cheerful laugh, dusting off a block. “Falling down is part of building! A solid pier needs three blocks side by side. Let's be steady engineers together!” With her help, the bridge stood taller and firmer.

分镜：Low angle building view: Teacher Zhang guides An'an and Tiantian's hands to set three base blocks securely; a sturdy double-level wooden bridge rises proudly.

### 第 10 页

玩得满头大汗的安安正擦脑门，江老师端着温水杯快步走了过来：“慢点喝，一小口一小口咽。”水温刚刚好，喝下去浑身舒坦。

Wiping sweat from her forehead, An'an felt thirsty. Teacher Jiang arrived promptly with a cup of warm water. “Sip slowly, one small swallow at a time.” The temperature was just right, soothing her from head to toe.

分镜：Close tender view: Teacher Jiang in lilac cardigan hands a small turquoise cup of water to flushed An'an; a clean folded towel rests over Teacher Jiang's wrist.

### 第 11 页

接着，江老师从围裙的大口袋里抽出一块柔软干燥的棉纱巾，轻巧地垫进安安的后脖颈：“出汗的后背不能吹风，垫上吸汗巾，跑跳才舒服。”

Next, Teacher Jiang pulled a soft cotton towel from her deep apron pocket and deftly tucked it inside the back of An'an's hoodie collar. “A sweaty back must stay warm. With this sweat cloth, you can play safely.”

分镜：Medium profile view: Teacher Jiang tucking a plain white cotton cloth behind An'an's neck inside her teal hoodie; An'an giggles with relaxed shoulders.

### 第 12 页

中午加餐时间，江老师推着亮堂堂的餐车走进来。南瓜浓汤盛在小瓷碗里，每一碗的分量都刚刚好。

At midday snack time, Teacher Jiang rolled in the spotless stainless steel cart. Golden pumpkin soup was poured into small ceramic bowls, each serving measured with loving care.

分镜：Dining table view: Teacher Jiang sets down a steaming small bowl of yellow soup before An'an; neat rows of small bowls on wooden tables, clean orderly classroom.

### 第 13 页

安安端碗时不小心把一滴汤洒在了桌角，她慌忙想用袖口去擦。江老师轻轻按住她的手腕，递来一块微湿的清洁布：“别弄脏新衣服，好孩子，拿抹布顺着桌边一擦就干净啦。”

When picking up her bowl, An'an accidentally dripped a drop of soup onto the table corner and hurried to wipe it with her sleeve. Teacher Jiang gently caught her wrist, offering a damp washcloth. “Don't stain your sleeves, sweetie. One wipe with the cloth makes it spotless.”

分镜：Tabletop close shot: Teacher Jiang gently hands a small clean cloth to An'an, preventing her teal sleeve from touching the table; An'an wipes the drop cleanly with relief.

### 第 14 页

午休时间到了。林老师拉上厚厚的鹅黄色遮光帘，张老师把小拖鞋一双双摆齐在床尾，江老师则挨个床铺轻轻掖好被角。三位老师走起路来，脚步都像小猫一样轻。

Naptime arrived. Teacher Lin drew the heavy curtains, Teacher Zhang aligned every pair of slippers at the bed ends, and Teacher Jiang tucked the quilt corners softly. The three teachers walked as silently as kittens.

分镜：Dim peaceful nap room: soft muted yellow glow behind curtains; three teachers softly moving between small wooden cots, tucking blankets and straightening little shoes.

### 第 15 页

安安躺在小床上，眼睛眨巴眨巴。江老师走过来，在床头放上装星星发夹的小碟子，轻声说：“闭上眼睛养养精神，江老师就在这儿守着大家呢。”安安听着心里踏实极了。

An'an lay on her little cot, her eyes blinking quietly. Teacher Jiang stepped over, set the little saucer for her yellow star clip on the nightstand, and whispered, “Just rest your eyes gently. I'm right here watching over you.” A cozy wave of calm washed over An'an.

分镜：Cot-side tender shot: Teacher Jiang tucks the daisy blanket under An'an's chin; the yellow star hair clip sits in a small white dish on low table; An'an's eyes soften toward sleep.

### 第 16 页

下午放学前，三位老师围坐在长桌旁整理一日记录：林老师记录下阅读角的互助故事，张老师挑出积木大桥的照片，江老师勾好每个人的喝水小打卡表。

Before afternoon dismissal, the three teachers sat together at the low table reviewing the day: Teacher Lin noted reading progress, Teacher Zhang selected photos of the giant bridge, and Teacher Jiang checked off water charts.

分镜：Afternoon desk scene: three teachers sitting together reviewing notebooks and checklists with gentle smiles; low wooden shelves and indoor plants behind them.

### 第 17 页

校门外妈妈在等候。林老师微笑着迎上前叮嘱明天要带的树叶，张老师对安安比了个大大的赞，江老师微笑着把整理好的橙色小书包递给安安。

Outside the door, Mom was waiting. Teacher Lin greeted her with a warm smile, Teacher Zhang gave An'an an energetic thumbs-up, and Teacher Jiang handed over the neatly zipped orange backpack.

分镜：Dismissal door view: Mom in coral cardigan holds An'an's hand; three teachers stand warmly by doorway; Teacher Lin speaks with Mom, Teacher Zhang winks with thumbs up, Teacher Jiang hands orange backpack.

### 第 18 页

走出大门，安安仰起头对妈妈说：“林老师像一盏明亮的台灯，张老师像一阵快乐的春风，江老师像一条暖和的厚围巾！有她们三个人，我们大班什么困难都不怕！”

Walking past the gate, An'an looked up at Mom with shining eyes. “Teacher Lin is like a bright desk lamp, Teacher Zhang is like a playful spring breeze, and Teacher Jiang is like a warm woolly scarf! With the three of them, our big class is ready for anything!”

分镜：Golden late-afternoon gate street: An'an in teal hoodie walks beside Mom looking up with lively joyful expression; soft imaginative warm glow in upper sky symbolizing lamp, breeze and cozy scarf.

共读问题：
- 安安在大班遇到不同的事情时，分别找了哪位老师帮忙？
- 林老师、张老师和江老师，各自有什么特别厉害的本领？

小活动：和孩子一起聊聊自己幼儿园班里的老师，画一张“我们的老师本领卡”，并试着在家里分工合作完成一件家务（如一人收碗、一人擦桌、一人倒垃圾）。
