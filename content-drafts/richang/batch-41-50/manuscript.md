## 41. 校门口的那一次挥手

副标题：把妈妈的爱装进口袋里

寓意：分开时的不舍很自然；装满安全感的小约定，能陪我们勇敢走进大班。

It is natural to feel reluctant when parting; a warm promise filled with love helps us step bravely into our day.

亲子目标：接纳入园分别时的依恋情绪，通过具体、可兑现的接送约定帮助孩子安心进入大班生活。

家长提醒：不用“都是大班孩子了还不懂事”羞辱难过，不偷偷溜走。分别仪式要温暖而简短，由成人坚守接园时间。

### 第 1 页

清晨，阳光洒在幼儿园大门前的台阶上。红色的欢迎拱门高高立着，许多小朋友正背着书包走进去。

Morning sunlight poured over the kindergarten steps. A welcoming archway stood tall as children walked in with their backpacks.

分镜：Wide sunny kindergarten entrance: colorful archway near stone steps, morning trees, An'an wearing orange backpack and Mom holding her hand; welcoming courtyard.

### 第 2 页

安安站在校门口的大树旁，脚步停住了。她拉着妈妈的手，手指抓得紧紧的。

An'an paused beside the big tree near the gate. She held Mom's hand, her small fingers gripping tightly.

分镜：Medium outdoor gate view: An'an stops near tree trunk holding Mom's hand tightly, hesitant watery eyes, orange backpack; Mom looks down with warm patient face.

### 第 3 页

“我已经上大班了。”安安小声说，可她还是把头靠在妈妈胳膊上。长大了一点，也还是会舍不得。

“I am in the oldest kindergarten class now,” An'an whispered, leaning against Mom's arm. Growing bigger did not mean she never missed Mom.

分镜：Medium portrait: An'an leans head against Mom's coral cardigan, reluctant expression, orange backpack straps visible; Mom stands gently beside her.

### 第 4 页

大班的教室在二楼，走廊比以前更长。安安看着那些高高的窗户，觉得幼儿园好像一下子变大了好多。

The senior classroom was on the second floor with longer hallways. Looking up at the tall windows, An'an felt the school had suddenly grown much bigger.

分镜：Low upward perspective: tall windows and kindergarten building above leafy trees; small An'an in teal hoodie looks up with uncertainty.

### 第 5 页

妈妈蹲下身来，把安安脸颊边的一缕碎发理到耳后，目光平平地看着她的眼睛。

Mom knelt down, gently brushed a loose strand of hair behind An'an's ear, and met her eyes at eye level.

分镜：Warm two-person eye-level medium shot: Mom kneels on clean pavement, tucking hair behind An'an's ear; yellow star hair clip gleams.

### 第 6 页

“安安是不是觉得，大班的事情又新又多，心里有点没准备好？”妈妈轻声问。

“Does the senior class feel big and new, making you feel a little unready inside?” Mom asked softly.

分镜：Close two-person conversation: Mom speaks with calm empathy at child height; An'an listens with moist eyes, nodding slightly.

### 第 7 页

安安点点头，声音小小的：“我想和你在一起。如果我想你了，怎么办呢？”

An'an nodded, her voice very quiet. “I want to stay with you. What if I miss you during the day?”

分镜：Close portrait: An'an looks at Mom with sincere emotion, lips pressed softly; Mom's listening hands rest gently on An'an's arms.

### 第 8 页

妈妈握住安安的小手，轻轻展开她的手掌。妈妈伸出食指，在安安的手心里画了一个小小的圆圈。

Mom took An'an's hand and gently opened her palm. With her forefinger, Mom drew a small circle in the center.

分镜：Close-up of hands: Mom's gentle finger traces an invisible circle in An'an's small open palm, warm morning light on skin.

### 第 9 页

“这是一颗隐形的小爱心。”妈妈微笑着按了按那块手心，“当你想妈妈时，就合上小手，把它按进心窝里。”

“This is a little invisible heart,” Mom smiled, pressing gently on her palm. “Whenever you miss me, close your hand and press it to your chest.”

分镜：Close two-person view: Mom presses An'an's palm softly; An'an looks down at her palm with curious wonder.

### 第 10 页

安安慢慢合拢手指，紧紧攥住手心里的温热，就像真的抓到了一颗红亮亮的小糖果。

An'an slowly curled her fingers into a fist, holding tight to the warmth in her palm as if gripping a shiny little sweet.

分镜：At the kindergarten entrance in morning light, An'an gently touches a curled fist to her chest, calming herself. Mom kneels at eye level in CORAL cardigan and CREAM trousers, reassures her warmly. One orange backpack worn by An'an. No gold sticker on forehead.

### 第 11 页

“下午离园时，我会在这棵树下等你。”妈妈指了指大树，“如果接送安排有变化，我会提前告诉老师。”

“I will wait under this tree at pick-up time,” Mom said. “If our plans change, I will let your teacher know beforehand.”

分镜：Medium view: Mom points toward leafy green tree near school gate, morning breeze rustling leaves; An'an listens attentively.

### 第 12 页

林老师从校门里迎出来，微笑着向安安伸出手：“早上好，安安！今天植物角的小番茄又长高了一点呢。”

Teacher Lin walked out from the gate, smiling as she offered a welcoming hand. “Good morning, An'an! The cherry tomatoes in our plant corner grew taller today.”

分镜：Three-person entrance scene: Teacher Lin in sage-green cardigan bends slightly to greet An'an; Mom stands beside, encouraging gently.

### 第 13 页

安安转头看看妈妈，妈妈微笑着点点头，松开了手：“去吧，安安。妈妈的爱就在你的口袋里。”

An'an looked back at Mom. Mom smiled and released her hand gently. “Go ahead, An'an. My love is right in your pocket.”

分镜：Medium shot: An'an takes one step forward toward Teacher Lin, looking back with brave expression; Mom waves with open relaxed posture.

### 第 14 页

安安跟着林老师走进楼里。在走廊的大窗户边，她停了一下，回过头去看。

An'an followed Teacher Lin inside. By the large corridor window, she paused and looked back.

分镜：Interior hallway view: An'an stands by bright corridor window looking down toward courtyard, clutching her backpack strap.

### 第 15 页

大树下，妈妈依然站在那里。妈妈抬起手，用力地、慢慢地向她挥动着手臂。

Under the big tree, Mom was still standing there, raising her arm and waving slowly and steadily toward the window.

分镜：Framed window view: outside in the sunny courtyard under the green tree, Mom waves with warm clear arm gesture.

### 第 16 页

安安把一只手贴在胸口，摸摸那颗“小爱心”，又举起另一只手，隔着玻璃朝妈妈挥了挥。

An'an pressed one hand to her chest over the little heart. With the other, she waved back through the glass.

分镜：Close emotional portrait: An'an smiles through the sunny window, hand waving high, yellow star hair clip sparkling in morning sun.

### 第 17 页

到了大班教室，阳光铺满地板。乐乐正在积木区招手：“安安，来帮我搭大吊桥！”安安走了过去。

Sunlight filled the classroom. Lele waved from the block corner. “An'an, come help me build a bridge!” She walked over.

分镜：Bright classroom wide shot: wooden shelves, reading rug, Lele sitting beside wooden blocks waving; An'an walks in cheerfully.

### 第 18 页

下午四点，放学铃声响起。安安背好书包飞奔出校门，大树下，妈妈果然张开双臂，正微笑着迎向她。

At four in the afternoon, the dismissal bell chimed. An'an ran out through the gate; under the big tree, Mom was waiting with open arms.

分镜：Warm afternoon closing shot: golden sunset glow under big tree, An'an runs toward smiling Mom with backpack bouncing happily.

共读问题：
- 妈妈在安安手心画了什么？
- 上学想家的时候，你有什么给自己打气的小办法？

小活动：和孩子一起设计一个专属的“告别击掌密码”或“口袋爱心”，并在入园前练习一次温和道别。

age4to5：允许孩子用指图、点头、手势等非语言方式表达；共读以倾听、感受安抚为主，不强求马上做到。

age6to8：讨论大班一日生活中的真实感受与解决策略，鼓励孩子自己提出小行动，家长承担托底支持与陪伴责任。

## 42. 摇晃的第一颗小乳牙

副标题：咬苹果的小秘密与长大的信号

寓意：身体的变化是在提醒我们悄悄长大；不必因为掉牙害怕或害羞。

Changes in our bodies remind us that we are growing; there is no need to feel afraid or shy about losing a baby tooth.

亲子目标：帮助 6 岁孩子认识乳牙脱落的自然生理过程，缓解对流血、疼痛和同伴眼光的焦虑。

家长提醒：换牙时间因人而异，不以是否掉牙判断孩子是否长大。不取笑缺牙或发音，不用线、工具强行拔牙。保持日常清洁；若明显疼痛、持续出血或受伤后松牙，由成人联系牙医。

### 第 1 页

上午加餐时间，桌上摆着一盘切好的红苹果片。安安拿起一块，咬了一大口。

During morning snack time, a plate of fresh red apple slices sat on the table. An'an took a piece and took a big bite.

分镜：Bright classroom snack table: wooden dining table with a plate of crisp apple slices; An'an holds an apple slice to her mouth.

### 第 2 页

“哎呀！”安安轻轻叫了一声，手里的半块苹果停在嘴边。她觉得下排中间的小牙齿晃了一下。

“Oh!” An'an gasped softly, pausing with the apple slice at her lips. She felt a lower front tooth wiggle slightly.

分镜：Close portrait: An'an pauses with apple slice near mouth, eyes wide with surprise, touching her lower lip gently.

### 第 3 页

安安把苹果放回盘子，停下来想了想。那颗小牙真的有点松，像一块快要松开的小积木。

An'an put the apple back on her plate and paused. The little tooth really did feel loose, like a block beginning to wobble.

分镜：Close-up: An'an's thoughtful face, tongue pressing subtly behind bottom lip, curious and cautious expression, plate nearby.

### 第 4 页

她轻轻托住脸颊，有点酸酸胀胀的。“牙齿怎么会摇晃呢？会不会掉下来？”安安心里怦怦直跳。

She rested her cheek in her hand. It felt a little tender. “Why is my tooth wobbly? Will it fall out?” Her heart beat faster.

分镜：Medium table shot: An'an sits alone at the table touching her lower jaw, slightly worried look, unfinished apple on plate.

### 第 5 页

乐乐端着水杯走过来，看见安安捂着嘴巴，笑着说：“安安，你是不是要掉牙变成说话漏风的小老头啦？”

Lele walked over with his water cup. Seeing An'an covering her mouth, he teased playfully: “Are you losing a tooth and going to whistle when you talk?”

分镜：Two-person table shot: Lele in mustard sweatshirt holds a water cup smiling; An'an quickly covers mouth with both hands, defensive and shy.

### 第 6 页

安安赶紧把嘴巴闭得紧紧的。一整个上午，她都不敢开口说话，生怕一张嘴牙齿就掉在地上。

An'an clamped her mouth shut tight. All morning, she hardly opened her lips, afraid the loose tooth might fall right onto the floor.

分镜：Quiet morning kindergarten reading corner. An'an sits alone on a small chair, a closed picture book resting in her lap, lips pressed tightly shut and eyebrows worried about her loose tooth. The book cover is plain muted green with a simple leaf illustration, NO lettering. No one laughs at her.

### 第 7 页

画画课上，老师请安安讲讲自己的小画，安安只是低着头指了指纸上的彩虹，声音小得像蚊子嗡嗡叫。

During art time, the teacher asked An'an about her drawing. An'an only pointed shyly at her painted rainbow, whispering like a tiny breeze.

分镜：Art table medium shot: Teacher Lin in sage-green cardigan bends to listen; An'an points timidly at rainbow drawing with mouth tightly shut.

### 第 8 页

下课后，林老师轻轻拍了拍安安的肩膀：“安安，今天嘴巴里是不是藏着一个小秘密？”

After class, Teacher Lin gently touched An'an's shoulder. “An'an, is there a little secret hiding in your mouth today?”

分镜：Quiet classroom corner: Teacher Lin sits beside An'an on small wooden chairs, gentle inquiring gaze, no other children around.

### 第 9 页

安安看了看四周，才慢慢张开嘴，指了指下门牙：“老师，我的牙齿摇晃了。它是不是坏掉了？”

An'an glanced around, then slowly opened her mouth and pointed to her bottom tooth. “Teacher, it's loose. Is it broken?”

分镜：Close two-person shot: An'an points to her lower front tooth with genuine concern; Teacher Lin looks closely with warm understanding smile.

### 第 10 页

林老师温和地说：“很多孩子长大时会换牙。我们一起告诉妈妈，留意这颗小牙，有不舒服再请牙医看看。”

“Many children lose baby teeth as they grow,” Teacher Lin said gently. “Let's tell Mom and watch this one. A dentist can help if it troubles you.”

分镜：Close portrait: Teacher Lin explains gently with warm gestures, pointing to an educational tooth model on low shelf nearby.

### 第 11 页

老师拿来图卡：“换牙时，小乳牙的牙根会慢慢被吸收，牙齿就松了。新恒牙也在牙龈下面慢慢准备着。”

分镜：Teacher Lin sits beside An'an in the reading corner, holding a small illustrated dental card; An'an listens with curiosity. No molar lobes, no long forked roots, no arrows or text. Small clear simple diagram, not a detailed medical chart. Kind calm explanation.

### 第 12 页

“每个人换牙的时间都不一样，不用和别人比。”林老师拿出一个粉色小盒子，“等小牙自然掉了，可以用它收藏。”

“Everyone loses teeth at their own time,” Teacher Lin said, offering a pink box. “When yours comes out naturally, you can keep it here.”

分镜：Teacher Lin offers the small keepsake box to An'an at the reading corner, warm encouraging expression. One small EMPTY rectangular translucent PINK hinged keepsake box with rounded corners and pale pink fabric lining, no tooth-shaped silhouette, no novelty tooth toy. ### 第 13 页

Holding the little box, An'an's fear gave way to curiosity. “Will it hurt when it comes out?”

分镜：Close portrait: An'an gently holds the OPEN small rectangular translucent pink keepsake box in both hands, looking up at Teacher Lin with a curious questioning expression. The box contains ONLY a pale pink soft pad; NO tooth yet. Teacher Lin's sage sleeve and calm face visible beside her.

### 第 14 页

“等它准备好了，让它自然掉下来，不要用力拔。”老师说，“每天轻轻刷牙，不舒服就告诉大人。”安安点点头。

“Let it come out when it is ready. Do not pull hard,” the teacher said. “Brush gently each day and tell a grown-up if it hurts.” An'an nodded.

午饭时，安安用后面的大牙慢慢嚼。乐乐走过来：“刚才的话让你难过了，对不起。”安安说：“换牙不是让人笑话的事。”

At lunch, An'an chewed slowly with her back teeth. Lele came over. “I'm sorry my joke upset you.” She said, “Losing a tooth is nothing to tease about.”

分镜：Lunch table scene: An'an sits with lunch bowl, speaking with gentle confidence to Lele; Lele looks surprised and curious.

### 第 16 页

过了几天，妈妈陪安安轻轻刷牙。那颗已经很松的小门牙自然掉了下来，落在洗手台边的白毛巾上。

A few days later, Mom stayed with An'an as she brushed gently. The very loose little front tooth came out naturally onto the white towel by the sink.

## 43. 拼音王国里的小弯钩

副标题：b 和 d 怎么又转晕啦？

寓意：刚学新符号分不清很正常；把身体当成小尺子，多试几次就能找到方向。

It is normal to confuse new symbols at first; using our hands and body makes finding the way fun and clear.

亲子目标：将抽象的拼音符号与具象身体动作连接，化解大班幼小衔接中的认读混淆与挫败感。

家长提醒：拼音是孩子感兴趣时的图卡游戏，不是大班必学、必写或入学测评要求。手势须成人与孩子同向，并明确手心朝自己；变换视角会镜像，不适合时直接看图卡。不要催写、比较速度或贴“笨、粗心”的标签。

### 第 1 页

大班的阅读角新添了彩色拼音图卡。安安觉得红红绿绿的小符号，像坐上小火车的乘客，想凑近看看。

New pinyin picture cards appeared in the kindergarten reading corner. Their colorful shapes reminded An'an of little train passengers. She wanted a closer look.

分镜：Bright classroom wall view: colorful chart with abstract rounded pinyin shapes on cork board; wooden desks and daylight from window.

### 第 2 页

安安对两个相像的符号感兴趣。林老师陪她念：“右下半圆 b，左下半圆 d。”旁边的小朋友也来看看。

Two similar shapes caught An'an's interest. Teacher Lin joined her: “The loop is on the right in b, and on the left in d.” Other children came to look.

分镜：Classroom front view: Teacher Lin points to wooden pointer near chart; children sitting at desks chanting together.

### 第 3 页

安安想照着图卡画一画。她拿起铅笔，看着四线三格纸：竖线直直的，可圆圈该画在哪一边呢？

An'an wanted to try copying the shapes. She picked up a pencil and looked at the lined paper. The stem was straight, but which side needed the loop?

分镜：Desk close-up: An'an holds yellow pencil above blank lined notebook, brow furrowed in deep thought; yellow star clip visible.

### 第 4 页

安安盯着本子看了半天，小脑袋歪向左边，又歪向右边。两个字母在脑子里转来转去，像转动的小陀螺。

An'an stared at her notebook, tilting her head left and then right. The two letters seemed to spin inside her mind like little tops.

分镜：Medium desk view: An'an tilts head with puzzled expression, eraser in left hand, pencil poised in right, blank lines on open book.

### 第 5 页

她一笔画下去，把圆圈画在了左边，可心里又觉得不对。拿橡皮擦掉，重新画在右边，怎么看还是觉得别扭。

She drew a circle on the left, but felt it was wrong. She erased it and drew it on the right, still feeling unsure.

分镜：Close overhead view: pencil marks erased leaving faint gray smudges on paper, pencil tip resting beside lines; child's hand uncertain.

### 第 6 页

旁边的小宇以前玩过这些图卡，已经画了一行。安安看看他的纸，再看看自己的，心里开始着急。

Xiaoyu had played with these cards before and had drawn a whole row. An'an looked from his paper to hers and began to worry.

分镜：Two-desk view: Xiaoyu in forest-green sweatshirt writes smoothly; An'an glances sideways at his notebook with a worried sigh.

### 第 7 页

安安急得吸了吸鼻子，手心开始冒出汗水。用力一擦，本子上的格子线被擦得模模糊糊，铅笔尖也“啪”的一声断掉了。

An'an grew anxious, her palms turning sweaty. She rubbed too hard, smudging the lines until her pencil lead snapped with a tiny snap.

“The park bench ahead has lovely sunshine. Let's sit for a while, shall we?” Mom suggested gently. An'an nodded in silence.

分镜：Street perspective: leafy sidewalk leading toward a sunlit wooden bench under golden autumn trees; mother and daughter walking softly.

### 第 7 页

两人坐在长椅上，先擦净双手。妈妈打开小保鲜盒，里面是两块小松饼。阳光暖暖的，安安也松了口气。

They sat on the bench and cleaned their hands. Mom opened a small container with two pancakes. In the warm sun, An'an relaxed a little.

分镜：Sunny park bench shot: Mom opens small food container, offering a warm golden pancake to An'an; green park lawn behind.

### 第 8 页

安安咬了一口甜甜的松饼，嚼得香喷喷的，肚子里暖和起来，整个人渐渐有了力气。

An'an took a bite of the sweet warm pancake. As warmth spread through her tummy, her energy began to return.

分镜：Close shot on bench: An'an munches happily on pancake, cheeks pinkening again in afternoon sunlight; Mom watches tenderly.

### 第 9 页

妈妈先说起自己的小插曲：“今天在办公室，我也遇见了一件没想到的事。”安安转过头来听。

Mom shared a little moment from her own day. “Something unexpected happened at work today.” An'an turned to listen.

分镜：Two-person bench conversation: Mom gestures comically with hands; An'an turns her head with growing amusement.

### 第 10 页

“我没拿稳杯子，几滴已经凉了的咖啡洒到鞋边。擦完才发现，还留了个小点。”妈妈指给她看。

“My cup slipped, and a few drops of cool coffee landed on my shoe. After wiping it, I found one little spot left.” Mom pointed it out.

分镜：Medium bench view: Mom lifts shoe showing a tiny coffee mark, laughing at herself; An'an's eyes sparkle with laughter.

### 第 11 页

“噗嗤——”安安笑了：“大人也有没拿稳的时候呀。”妈妈也笑起来，母女俩慢慢轻松了。

An'an gave a little laugh. “Grown-ups drop things too!” Mom smiled, and they both began to feel at ease.

分镜：Close portrait: An'an giggles with hand over mouth, pancake in other hand, eyes creased with pure happiness.

### 第 12 页

咽下最后一口松饼，安安自己主动拉了拉妈妈的袖子：“妈妈，我今天在学校也有一件超好笑的事想告诉你！”

Finishing her pancake, An'an tugged Mom's sleeve on her own: “Mom, something super funny happened in my class today too!”

分镜：Warm two-person moment: An'an turns excitedly toward Mom, gesturing animatedly with her small hands.

### 第 13 页

安安接着讲：“乐乐画了一条长长的毛毛虫，大家却看成小火车！他给它添了车轮，真的开进故事里啦。”

An'an continued, “Lele drew a long caterpillar, but we thought it was a train! He added wheels, and it really became part of our story.”

分镜：Medium bench shot: An'an talks with joyful animation, describing the scene with gestures; Mom listens with chin on hand, beaming.

### 第 14 页

“还有呢，我今天第一次当了大班值日生，给绿萝喷了水！老师还给我额头贴了一颗金色大星星呢！”安安指了指额头。

“And I was duty helper today, spraying the pothos plants! The teacher put a gold star right on my forehead!” An'an pointed to her brow.

分镜：Close view: An'an points proudly to the shiny gold star sticker on her temple, telling the story with sparkling eyes.

### 第 15 页

妈妈认真听着，轻轻点头：“后来呢？”她没有急着评价，也没有追问，让安安按自己的顺序说。

Mom listened and nodded gently. “What happened next?” She did not rush to judge or ask more questions, letting An'an tell it her way.

分镜：Two-person medium shot: Mom gazes at An'an with complete loving attention, hair glowing in sunset backlighting.

### 第 16 页

夕阳把影子拉长了。安安牵着妈妈的手回家，今天想说的话都有了位置。哪天想先安静一会儿，也可以慢慢来。

Their shadows grew long as they walked home hand in hand. Today's stories had room to be heard. On another day, quiet could come first too.

分镜：Warm closing wide shot: mother and daughter walk hand-in-hand along tree-shaded sidewalk toward home, bathed in golden dusk light.

共读问题：
- 妈妈在长椅上先跟安安分享了什么趣事？
- 今天放学路上，你最想跟爸爸妈妈聊哪一件开心的小事？

小活动：开启晚餐或睡前的“今天的三件小事”时光：轮流说出一件开心的事、一件不容易的事和一件感谢的事。

age4to5：允许孩子用指图、点头、手势等非语言方式表达；共读以倾听、感受安抚为主，不强求马上做到。

age6to8：讨论大班一日生活中的真实感受与解决策略，鼓励孩子自己提出小行动，家长承担托底支持与陪伴责任。
