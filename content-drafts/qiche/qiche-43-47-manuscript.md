## 无人机，把快递送上天啦？

社区低空智慧物流与空中走廊

### 第 1 页

星期六的早晨，阳光洒在绿意盎然的未来社区广场上。安安背着橙色小背包，手里抱着小熊，和妈妈一起散步去社区的智慧物流驿站取包裹。

**English**

On Saturday morning, sunshine fell across the green plaza of their futuristic neighborhood. An'an carried her orange backpack, held Bear in her arms, and walked with Mom to the smart delivery hub to pick up a parcel.

### 第 2 页

刚走到驿站开阔的草坪前，安安就听见头顶传来一阵轻柔平稳的“嗡嗡”声。她抬起头，惊喜地指着半空：“妈妈，快看！天上有一只长着四个螺旋桨的‘大蜻蜓’！”

**English**

Just as they reached the open lawn before the hub, An'an heard a soft, steady hum above. She looked up and pointed into the air with delight. “Mom, look! There's a giant dragonfly with four propellers in the sky!”

### 第 3 页

那是一架白橙相间的四旋翼物流无人机。它既没有像小鸟一样拍打翅膀，也没有像普通飞机那样快速向前滑行，而是稳稳当当地停在半空中。

**English**

It was a white-and-orange quadcopter delivery drone. It neither flapped wings like a bird nor zoomed forward like a regular airplane; instead, it hovered steadily in midair.

### 第 4 页

身穿深蓝色工作马甲的物流工程师林阿姨走过来，微笑着向安安招手。安安好奇地问：“林阿姨，无人机停在天上，为什么不会掉下来呢？”

**English**

Wearing her dark-blue work vest, logistics engineer Lin walked over and waved to An'an with a smile. An'an asked curiously, “Auntie Lin, why doesn't the drone fall down when it stays still in the sky?”

### 第 5 页

林阿姨蹲下身，指着头顶旋转的旋翼：“你看，四个旋翼都在飞快地旋转，把空气用力往下推。空气就会产生向上的反推力，正好稳稳地把无人机托在空中。”

**English**

Auntie Lin knelt down and pointed to the spinning rotors above. “Look, all four rotors spin rapidly, pushing air firmly downward. The air pushes back with an upward lift, holding the drone steadily aloft.”

### 第 6 页

安安看着小旋翼若有所思：“那如果它想往前飞，或者拐个弯去另一条街，该怎么做呀？”

**English**

An'an looked thoughtfully at the small rotors. “Then what does it do if it wants to fly forward or turn around toward another street?”

### 第 7 页

林阿姨拿出一个不会转动的教学模型：“电脑会调节各个旋翼的转速，让机身朝需要的方向微微倾斜，就能向前或向旁边飞。转弯时，还要配合调整旋翼的转动。”

**English**

Auntie Lin brought out a model with motionless blades. “The computer adjusts the rotor speeds to tilt the drone in the direction it needs to travel. Coordinated changes to the rotors also help it turn.”

### 第 8 页

安安仰头望向周围的高楼和大树，有些担心地说：“城市里有那么多高楼、路灯和大树，无人机飞在天上，会不会撞到电线呀？”

**English**

An'an looked up at the nearby tall buildings and trees, feeling a bit worried. “There are so many towers, streetlights, and trees in our city. Won't the drone bump into wires while flying?”

### 第 9 页

“这架无人机装着帮助观察的‘眼睛’。”林阿姨指着展示模型上的镜头，“相机和激光雷达会收集周围的信息，再交给电脑判断。不同的无人机，设备也可能不一样。”

**English**

“This drone has sensors that help it look around.” Auntie Lin pointed to the lenses on the display model. “Cameras and lidar gather information for its computer. Other drones may use different equipment.”

### 第 10 页

“发现障碍物时，系统会尝试减速、停住或改变路线。不过，细电线和突然出现的小鸟也可能很难发现，所以工作人员还要提前检查路线，持续看护飞行。”

**English**

“When it detects an obstacle, the system may slow down, stop, or change course. Thin wires and birds that appear suddenly can still be hard to detect, so staff check the route beforehand and monitor the flight.”

### 第 11 页

林阿姨指着驿站的路线示意图：“这条配送线路提前规划好了，要绕开不适合飞行的地方。图里的绿色线只是帮我们看懂路线，真实天空里可没有发光的道路哦。”

**English**

Auntie Lin pointed to the hub's route display. “This delivery route is planned in advance to avoid unsuitable places. The green lines help us understand the map; real roads in the sky do not glow.”

### 第 12 页

这时，远处起降区的无人机接到降落指令，平稳地垂直下降。安安和妈妈留在护栏与黄色安全线外，林阿姨提醒：“我们就在参观区静静看，不能靠近旋翼。”

**English**

The drone in the separate landing area began descending vertically. An'an and Mom stayed behind the barrier and yellow safety line. “Let's watch from the visitor area and keep clear of the rotors,” Auntie Lin reminded them.

### 第 13 页

无人机的四个起落架稳稳落在一个圆形的起降平台上，旋翼慢慢停止了旋转。紧接着，起降台下方的投递口自动打开，包裹被平稳地送进了智能快递柜。

**English**

The drone's four landing gears settled firmly onto a circular landing pad, and its rotors slowly came to a halt. Right after, a hatch beneath the pad opened automatically, lowering the parcel into a smart locker.

### 第 14 页

叮咚！妈妈的手机收到了包裹已到柜的通知。她在柜机前出示取件码，一个小柜门弹开了，里面是装着新科学画册的包裹。

**English**

Ding-dong! Mom's phone announced that the parcel was ready. She showed her pickup code at the locker, and a small door opened. Inside was the parcel containing An'an's new science picture book.

### 第 15 页

安安抱着新画册，开心地向林阿姨挥手道谢。林阿姨笑着说：“出发前还要检查电量和天气。遇到不适合飞行的大风或雷雨，配送就要暂停，不能勉强起飞。”

**English**

Holding her new book, An'an waved goodbye to Auntie Lin. “Before a flight, we check the battery and weather too. Strong winds or thunderstorms can mean pausing deliveries instead of taking off,” Auntie Lin said.

### 第 16 页

回到家，安安找出一只小纸风车，鼓起腮帮子用力一吹，风车呼啦啦飞转起来。她把小熊放在积木拼成的小停机坪旁，自豪地说：“等我长大了，也要设计一架聪明的送货小飞侠！”

**English**

Back home, An'an took out a small paper pinwheel and puffed her cheeks to blow on it, making the blades whirl around. She placed Bear beside a toy landing pad built with blocks and said proudly, “When I grow up, I will design a smart flying helper too!”

### 亲子共读

共读目标：认识多旋翼通过推动空气获得升力，了解传感器、预先规划的配送路线和工作人员监控各自的作用；知道避障能力有局限。

家长提醒：本书的自动收件平台是故事中的示范设施，不代表每个社区都有。儿童由成人陪同在护栏外参观；纸风车只能帮助观察气流与转动，不能证明风车能载重起飞。

可以聊一聊：

- 无人机悬停时，四个旋翼在做什么？
- 有了传感器，为什么还需要检查路线和天气？
- 什么情况下地面配送更合适，什么情况下可以尝试无人机配送？

桌面小游戏：在成人陪同下转动现成的纸风车，观察空气与叶片的互动；用积木、纸盒搭一个静止的配送驿站，讲述包裹从平台到柜子的路线。不连接电机，不接近真实飞行器。

4–5 岁：寻找画面里的四个小旋翼、黄色安全线与智能快递柜，模仿无人机起飞与平稳降落的声音。

6–8 岁：探讨城市“低空航道”为什么需要规划路线，对比地面快递货车与空中物流无人机各自的优点和天气局限。

## 高铁小飞龙，怎样平稳跑得快？

现代综合枢纽与流线型平稳科技

### 第 1 页

阳光明媚的上午，妈妈开着浅蓝色小汽车，带安安前往城市东侧新建的综合交通客运枢纽。安安系着儿童安全带，望着车窗外飞速后退的高大绿树。

**English**

On a sunny morning, Mom drove their light-blue car, taking An'an toward the city's newly built integrated passenger transport hub on the eastern side. Buckled in her safety seat, An'an watched tall green trees glide past the window.

### 第 2 页

小汽车平稳地驶入枢纽的地下停车场。安安拉着妈妈的手乘直梯来到明亮的换乘大厅。头顶的巨大电子屏上，滚动的列车信息像瀑布一样清晰。

**English**

The car rolled smoothly into the hub's underground parking garage. Holding Mom's hand, An'an took the elevator up into the bright transfer concourse. On the massive overhead screen, train information scrolled clearly like a waterfall.

### 第 3 页

“妈妈，这里真热闹呀！”安安看着换乘示意图，“地下有地铁，外面有公交，高铁站台在另一层。跟着指引，就能去换乘啦！”

**English**

“Mom, this place is so busy!” An'an studied the transfer map. “The subway is below, buses are outside, and high-speed trains are on another level. The signs help us find the way between them!”

### 第 4 页

今天是车站的铁路科普开放日。工作人员核验预约后，带安安和妈妈来到指定参观位置。妈妈牵好她的手：“不同交通工具在这里相连，换乘就方便多啦。”

**English**

It was the station's railway science open day. After checking their reservation, a staff member led An'an and Mom to the designated viewing area. Mom held her hand. “Connecting different kinds of transport makes changing between them easier.”

### 第 5 页

站台旁，一列洁白修长的高铁动车组静静地停在轨道上。银白色的车身在天顶洒下的阳光里闪烁着柔和的光芒，车身侧面绘着优雅的深蓝色流线饰条。

**English**

Beside the platform, a sleek, long white bullet train rested quietly on the tracks. Its silver-white body gleamed gently beneath the skylight, decorated with elegant deep-blue streamlined stripes along the sides.

### 第 6 页

安安睁大了好奇的眼睛：“妈妈，你看它的车头尖尖的、长长的，圆润又光滑，真像一条准备展翅飞翔的银色小龙！为什么它的脑袋不是方方的呢？”

**English**

An'an widened her curious eyes. “Mom, look at the nose—pointed, long, rounded, and smooth, just like a little silver dragon ready to take flight! Why isn't its head square like an ordinary bus?”

### 第 7 页

站台上佩戴对讲机、身穿藏青色制服的列车长方阿姨微笑着走过来：“小朋友问得真好！当高铁以每小时三百多公里的超快速度飞奔时，前面的空气会像一堵厚厚的墙一样挡住它。”

**English**

Wearing a dark-navy uniform and carrying a walkie-talkie, Conductor Fang walked over with a smile. “What a great question, little one! When the bullet train rushes ahead at over three hundred kilometers per hour, the air ahead pushes back like a thick wall.”

### 第 8 页

方阿姨从口袋里拿出一枚小书签给安安比划：“如果是方头大车，空气就会迎面撞击；而超长流线型的尖车头，能像锋利又柔和的小船划开水面一样，把迎面而来的空气推向两侧，大大减少空气阻力。”

**English**

Conductor Fang took a bookmark from her pocket to demonstrate. “A blunt front would crash straight into the air; but a long streamlined nose glides through the air like a sleek boat parting water, pushing airflow smoothly aside and cutting air resistance way down.”

### 第 9 页

安安仰起头，看到高铁车顶上有一根银色折叠的金属“手臂”，顶端紧紧贴在上方的高压电线上：“那根银色的小手臂，是在和电线牵手吗？”

**English**

An'an tilted her head up and spotted a silver folding metal arm on the train roof, pressing firmly against the overhead wire. “Is that little silver arm holding hands with the wire?”

### 第 10 页

“那叫受电弓。”方阿姨说，“它把接触网的电引进列车，经过车上的设备，再送到牵引电机，让车轮转起来。上面的电线不能靠近，更不能碰。”

**English**

“That is the pantograph,” Conductor Fang said. “It collects electricity from the overhead contact wire. Equipment on the train supplies the traction motors, which turn the wheels. We must stay well away from those wires.”

### 第 11 页

“这列高铁用电运行，车上不用像蒸汽火车那样烧煤。”方阿姨接着说，“电也要节约。它从哪里来、怎样发出来，同样关系到环境。”

**English**

“This train runs on electricity instead of burning coal on board like a steam train,” Conductor Fang continued. “We still need to save energy. How electricity is produced matters to the environment too.”

### 第 12 页

安安站在参观区，望着向远处延伸的铁轨：“老火车故事里总有‘咣当、咣当’的声音。高铁的轨道，也是一小段一小段拼起来的吗？”

**English**

From the viewing area, An'an looked at the rails stretching into the distance. “Old train stories always go clackety-clack. Are high-speed railway tracks made from lots of short pieces too?”

### 第 13 页

方阿姨指着展示模型：“许多钢轨会焊成很长的一段，接头还要细细打磨。这样的长钢轨，能减少车轮经过接头时的冲击。”

**English**

Conductor Fang pointed to a display model. “Many rails are welded into long lengths, and the joints are carefully ground smooth. This reduces the bumps as wheels pass over the joints.”

### 第 14 页

“不过，高铁也不是一点声音和晃动都没有。”方阿姨说，“平顺的轨道、车底的减振装置和认真养护，一起帮助列车跑得更稳。”

**English**

“High-speed trains still make sounds and can move or sway,” Conductor Fang said. “Smooth tracks, suspension under the cars, and careful maintenance all help make the ride steadier.”

### 第 15 页

站台安全防线提示音响起。方阿姨温和地提醒安安和妈妈：“列车马上要关门发车了，请站在白色安全线后方，注意列车启动时的空气气流哦。”

**English**

The platform chime sounded. Conductor Fang gently reminded An'an and Mom, “The train doors are about to close. Please stand behind the white safety line to stay safe from the moving air currents as the train departs.”

### 第 16 页

关门提示音响起，列车合上车门。银色小飞龙缓缓启动，带着轻轻的运行声滑出站台，沿着高架轨道驶向远方。

**English**

The door chime sounded, and the doors closed. The silver dragon started gently and rolled out of the station with a soft running sound, following the elevated railway toward the distance.

### 第 17 页

展示屏播放了一段平稳运行时的视频：车厢里，窗台上的一枚硬币暂时立住了！安安睁大眼睛。方阿姨提醒：“这是一次演示，硬币并不是任何时候都不会倒哦。”

**English**

A display showed a video taken during a smooth part of a journey: a coin briefly balanced on its edge on a carriage windowsill. An'an's eyes widened. “This is one demonstration; a coin will not stay upright all the time,” Conductor Fang reminded her.

### 第 18 页

安安点点头：“尖车头帮助它减少风阻，轨道和减振装置帮助它跑得稳！”方阿姨笑了：“还有驾驶员、调度人员和检修人员一起合作呢。”

**English**

An'an nodded. “The streamlined nose helps reduce air resistance, and the tracks and suspension help it run steadily!” Conductor Fang smiled. “Drivers, controllers, and maintenance teams work together too.”

### 第 19 页

安安牵着妈妈，留在安全线内侧的参观区向远去的高铁挥手。银色小飞龙沿着轨道越走越远，载着旅客驶向另一座城市。

**English**

Holding Mom's hand, An'an stayed in the visitor area on the safe side of the line and waved goodbye. The silver dragon followed the tracks into the distance, carrying travelers toward another city.

### 第 20 页

走出车站，安安抱着小熊，开心地对妈妈说：“下次放小长假，我们一定要坐上这列平稳的银色小飞龙，去外婆家的大草原看风景！”

**English**

Leaving the station, An'an hugged Bear and said cheerfully to Mom, “On our next holiday, let's ride this smooth silver dragon all the way to Grandma's house to see the wide grasslands!”

### 亲子共读

共读目标：认识综合交通枢纽、流线外形、受电弓和长钢轨的作用，区分减小风阻、提供动力与改善平稳性。

家长提醒：真实站台需按车站规则进入，本书是有预约和工作人员引导的科普开放日。始终牵手站在安全线内侧，不蹲靠站台边，不触碰轨道和接触网。硬币是视频中的短暂演示，不代表列车永不晃动。

可以聊一聊：

- 尖尖的车头怎样帮助空气从列车旁边流过？
- 受电弓收来的电，最后交给什么部件？
- 除了轨道，还有谁和哪些设备帮助列车平稳运行？

桌面小游戏：成人准备两段硬纸板，一段平整，一段有轻微接缝。孩子在桌面慢慢推动玩具车，感受接缝带来的变化。说明这只演示路面平顺性的一个因素，真实列车还需要悬挂减振和养护。

4–5 岁：找一找尖车头、受电弓和安全线，听一听列车的运行声音。

6–8 岁：区分空气阻力、动力、轨道平顺性与悬挂减振，不把一次硬币演示当成所有乘车情况。

## 大飞机，在跑道上排队吗？

智慧空港地面协同与机翼升力起飞

### 第 1 页

穿过综合客运枢纽宽敞明亮的空中玻璃连廊，安安和妈妈来到了空港新城的机场观景科普大厅。这里的整面墙都是巨大透明的隔音玻璃窗。

**English**

Crossing the spacious glass skywalk from the integrated transport hub, An'an and Mom arrived at the airport observatory hall in the airport new town. The entire wall here was made of huge, crystal-clear soundproof windows.

### 第 2 页

午后的阳光照在辽阔平坦的水泥停机坪上。远处，几架雪白修长的大客机整齐地停靠在登机桥边。安安快步跑到观景窗前，惊叹地睁大了眼睛：“妈妈，大飞机近看比绘本里还要巨大呢！”

**English**

Afternoon sunshine bathed the vast, flat concrete apron. In the distance, several sleek white airliners rested neatly beside passenger boarding bridges. An'an ran to the window and marveled with wide eyes. “Mom, up close, big airplanes look even more gigantic than in picture books!”

### 第 3 页

安安仔细打量着其中一架客机，发现飞机身边围着好多形状奇特的小汽车，工人们正在忙碌地穿梭操作。

**English**

An'an looked closely at one of the airliners and noticed many oddly shaped utility vehicles bustling around it while ground crew worked busily.

### 第 4 页

一辆车身扁扁的、四个轮子特别粗壮的黄色矮卡车，正缓缓倒车来到飞机的机头下方，用坚固的特制连接杆稳稳夹住了飞机的前起落架。

**English**

A low yellow truck with a flat body and extra-thick tires slowly backed underneath the plane's nose, firmly latching onto the front landing gear with a heavy-duty tow bar.

### 第 5 页

杜叔叔走到观景窗旁：“那是飞机牵引车。客机不能像小汽车一样挂倒挡，在这种靠着登机桥的机位，通常请牵引车帮忙向后推出。”

**English**

Uncle Du came to the viewing window. “That is an aircraft tug. An airliner cannot select a reverse gear like a car. At gates like this, a tug usually helps push it backward away from the boarding bridge.”

### 第 6 页

登机桥和保障车辆都退开后，工作人员按程序指挥牵引车，把客机慢慢向后推出机位。安安发现：原来出发前，每个伙伴都要先做好自己的事。

**English**

After the boarding bridge and service vehicles had moved clear, the ground crew directed the tug to push the airliner slowly backward from its stand. An'an noticed how each helper had a job to finish before departure.

### 第 7 页

**English**

Another aircraft nearby was still preparing: a belt loader carried bags into its cargo hold, and a truck supplied fuel beneath its wing. “Those jobs must be completed before pushback,” Uncle Du explained.

**English**

### 第 9 页

**English**

### 第 10 页

**English**

**English**

Once the tug disconnected, the white airliner rolled smoothly along the taxiway toward the main runway. Its long wings stretched gently under the sun, tilting upward with graceful wingtips.

### 第 12 页

安安托着小下巴，认真地问：“杜叔叔，大飞机肚子里装着几百位乘客和那么多沉重的行李，它到底是怎么被托上天空的呢？”

**English**

Resting her chin in her hands, An'an asked thoughtfully, “Uncle Du, with hundreds of passengers and heavy luggage inside, how does such a heavy plane get lifted into the sky?”

### 第 13 页

**English**

### 第 14 页

**English**

**English**

**English**

**English**

“Look! Rotation!” Uncle Du pointed into the distance. The nose lifted gently, the nose gear left the ground, and moments later the main landing gear floated free as the airliner soared into the blue sky like a graceful swan!

### 第 18 页

**English**

An'an watched without blinking. As the plane climbed higher, its landing gear folded neatly into the belly, and the bay doors sealed shut, leaving the fuselage completely sleek and smooth.

### 第 19 页

杜叔叔说：“起落架收起来，能减少这部分空气阻力。机翼、发动机和飞行员的操作，还要继续配合，帮助飞机向上爬升。”

**English**

Uncle Du said, “Retracting the landing gear reduces the drag it creates. The wings, engines, and pilots continue working together as the aircraft climbs.”

### 第 20 页

白色大飞机越飞越远，变成蓝天里一个小小的亮点。安安抱紧小熊：“这么多地面和空中的伙伴一起合作，大飞机才能出发呀！”

**English**

The white airliner grew smaller until it was a tiny bright speck in the blue sky. An'an hugged Bear. “So many helpers on the ground and in the air work together to send it on its way!”

## 天上的卫星，怎么给汽车引路？

航天发射与指引千家万户的太空北斗

### 第 1 页

下午，妈妈带安安来到航天科技馆。刚迈进高大的序厅，一座白色运载火箭模型就映入眼帘。安安仰起头：“这么大的火箭，能带什么去太空？”

**English**

That afternoon, Mom took An'an to the aerospace museum. A tall white model of a launch vehicle filled the entrance hall. An'an looked up. “What can such a big rocket carry into space?”

### 第 2 页

火箭高高耸立，笔直指向天花板上的璀璨星空图景。安安仰起小脑瓜，紧紧抓着妈妈的衣角：“妈妈，火箭长得好高好壮呀！它要飞到天上去找谁呢？”

**English**

The rocket stood tall, pointing straight toward a sparkling starry sky painted across the ceiling. An'an tilted her little head up and held Mom's cardigan tightly. “Mom, the rocket is so tall and strong! Who is it flying up to find?”

### 第 3 页

身穿淡蓝色科技馆讲解服的梁阿姨微笑着走过来：“小朋友，运载火箭就像宇宙里的‘超级货车’。它的本领就是克服地球引力，把各种卫星、飞船和探测器稳稳送入太空轨道。”

**English**

Wearing her light-blue museum blazer, docent Auntie Liang walked over with a smile. “Hello little explorer! A carrier rocket is like a cosmic cargo truck. Its mission is to overcome Earth's gravity, delivering satellites, spacecraft, and probes safely into space orbits.”

### 第 4 页

梁阿姨打开展示屏：“火箭能把导航卫星送上预定轨道。北斗就是一套卫星导航系统，许多卫星在不同轨道上工作，为地面提供定位信号。”

**English**

Auntie Liang opened a display. “Rockets can put navigation satellites into planned orbits. BeiDou is a satellite navigation system with spacecraft in different orbits, providing positioning signals to Earth.”

### 第 5 页

安安看着屏幕上闪烁的小光点，歪了歪头：“导航卫星在那么高、那么远的太空里，它又是怎样和我们地面的小汽车交朋友的呢？”

**English**

Looking at the twinkling dots of light on screen, An'an tilted her head. “Navigation satellites are so high and far away in space—how do they make friends with the cars down on our streets?”

### 第 6 页

梁阿姨指着透明汽车模型：“这件展品把导航天线和接收设备的位置展示出来了。天线像‘耳朵’，接收设备和电脑一起处理信号。真实汽车的安装位置可能不同。”

**English**

Auntie Liang pointed to the transparent car model. “This exhibit shows its navigation antenna and receiver. The antenna is like an ear, while the receiver and computer process the signals. Their positions can differ in real cars.”

### 第 7 页

“太空中有多颗导航卫星时刻向地球发射看不见的无线电电波。这些电波就像带着时间戳的小信使，以光一样的速度飞向地面。”

**English**

“Multiple navigation satellites in space broadcast invisible radio waves down to Earth every second. These waves travel at the speed of light like little messengers carrying precise timestamps.”

### 第 8 页

“通常，接收设备需要至少四颗卫星的有效信号，才能一起计算自己的位置和时钟误差。地图软件再把这个位置放到地图上，帮我们规划路线。”

**English**

“Usually a receiver needs usable signals from at least four satellites to calculate its position and clock error together. Map software then places that position on a map and helps plan a route.”

### 第 9 页

安安开心地拍起手：“我懂啦！卫星送来定位信号，导航设备和地图软件一起算路，才有屏幕上的蓝箭头。卫星并不是直接在天上喊‘右转’呀！”

**English**

An'an clapped happily. “I get it! Satellites send positioning signals, and the receiver and map software work out the route for the blue arrow. The satellites aren't calling down ‘turn right’ from space!”

### 第 10 页

梁阿姨点点头：“汽车、轮船和许多飞行器会用卫星导航辅助定位。高铁也有相关应用，不过列车控制和安全运行，还需要轨道信号等许多设备共同配合。”

**English**

Auntie Liang nodded. “Cars, ships, and many aircraft use satellite navigation to help locate themselves. Railways use it in some applications too, while train control and safety also rely on trackside signaling and other equipment.”

### 第 11 页

在互动沙盘上，安安把城市切换成黑夜，又选了雨天。导航还能工作。再把车开进模型隧道，定位提示变了。梁阿姨说：“建筑和隧道可能挡住信号，定位也会有误差。”

**English**

At the interactive model, An'an chose night and then rain, and navigation still worked. When she moved the car into a model tunnel, its positioning indicator changed. “Buildings and tunnels can block signals, and positions can have errors,” Auntie Liang explained.

### 第 12 页

“导航只是帮助认路，不能替驾驶员看路。”梁阿姨说，“遇到雨雾、信号不好或地图与现场不一致时，要观察真实道路和标志，按情况减速、停车或调整路线。”

**English**

“Navigation helps with directions; it cannot see the road for the driver,” Auntie Liang said. “In rain or fog, with poor signals, or when a map differs from the road, drivers must observe the real road and signs and respond to conditions.”

### 第 13 页

接着，他们参观天宫空间站模型。梁阿姨说：“空间站让航天员生活和做实验，导航卫星负责播发定位信号。它们都在太空，却有不同的任务。”

**English**

Next they visited a model of the Tiangong space station. “The station is a place for astronauts to live and work. Navigation satellites broadcast positioning signals,” Auntie Liang said. “Both are in space, but they have different jobs.”

### 第 14 页

安安抱着小熊，学着航天员的样子敬了个礼：“谢谢你们，把这么多问题变成新发现！”她又想看看，太空的本领还能怎样帮助城市生活。

**English**

Holding Bear, An'an gave an astronaut-style salute. “Thank you for turning so many questions into discoveries!” She wondered what else space technology could help people do on Earth.

### 第 15 页

参观结束时，梁阿姨给安安的参观手册上盖了一枚金色的“小小航天探索者”印章。安安宝贝地收好手册，紧紧牵着妈妈的手走出了科技馆。

**English**

At the end of the visit, Auntie Liang stamped An'an's booklet with a golden badge: “Little Space Explorer.” An'an carefully put the booklet away and held Mom's hand tightly as they walked out of the museum.

### 第 16 页

坐回后排儿童座椅，系好安全带，安安望向关好的玻璃天窗。妈妈停稳车、设好下一站的路线：“我们再去未来出行体验馆看看。”安安笑着点点头。

**English**

Buckled into her rear child seat, An'an looked up through the closed glass sunroof. With the car parked, Mom set their next route. “Let's visit the future mobility pavilion next.” An'an smiled and nodded.

### 亲子共读

共读目标：区分火箭运送、导航卫星广播、接收设备定位、地图软件规划路线与空间站实验等不同任务，知道卫星定位也有遮挡和误差。

家长提醒：卫星定位不是从太空拍到汽车，也不是卫星直接发送转弯口令。至少四颗卫星的有效信号用于通常的三维位置与接收机时钟求解；建筑、隧道等会影响接收。车辆导航不能保证恶劣天气下安全，始终观察现场。

夕阳照亮了科技园的玻璃展馆。安安和妈妈把浅蓝色小汽车停好，步行来到今天的最后一站——未来低空出行体验馆。

### 第 3 页

### 第 6 页

赵叔叔指着旋翼：“它不需要像大客机那样长距离滑跑，能在合适的起降场垂直升降。不过，起降场仍要留出足够空间，还要检查天气、电量和飞行条件。”

**English**

Uncle Zhao pointed to the rotors. “It can rise and descend vertically at a suitable landing site without an airliner's long takeoff roll. It still needs enough clear space, along with checks of weather, battery charge, and flight conditions.”

### 第 7 页

**English**

“Different designs move forward in different ways,” Uncle Zhao said, opening a concept animation. “Some tilt their rotors to direct thrust forward. As they gain speed, wings can provide lift too. The method depends on the aircraft's design.”

### 第 8 页

**English**

Looking at the dense apartment buildings outside, An'an asked worriedly, “If it flies above city buildings, won't the engines keep everyone awake? Will it blow out smelly exhaust smoke?”

### 第 9 页

**English**

### 第 10 页

赵叔叔指着未来城市模型：“设计者希望用它连接合适的起降场，帮助一些跨江、跨海湾的短途出行。哪些地方能飞、能带多少人、能飞多远，都需要检验和管理。”

**English**

Uncle Zhao pointed to a future city model. “Designers hope to connect suitable landing sites for some short journeys across rivers or bays. Where it can fly, how many people it can carry, and how far it can go all need testing and oversight.”

### 第 11 页

赵叔叔拉开车门，邀请安安和妈妈坐进静态体验舱：“来，坐进来体验一下未来的驾驶舱吧！”安安乖巧地坐上座椅，系好了四点式安全带。

**English**

### 第 12 页

舱内没有密密麻麻的复杂机械仪表，只有一块超大的一体化弧面智能液晶屏。大屏幕缓缓点亮，模拟出未来城市的立体航道景观。

**English**

Inside there were no cluttered mechanical dials, only a massive curved smart display. The screen lit up gently, simulating the 3D skyways of a futuristic city.

### 第 13 页

戴上轻便的沉浸式眼镜，安安仿佛真的乘着小飞车腾空而起！脚下是一座充满生机的立体未来之城：地下是飞速穿梭的地铁，地面是平稳奔驰的高铁与电动车。

**English**

Putting on lightweight immersive goggles, An'an felt as if she were soaring into the clouds! Below spread a vibrant future metropolis: subways darting underground, bullet trains and electric cars gliding smoothly on the surface.

### 第 14 页

模拟画面里，彩色线条帮助安安看懂不同航线。赵叔叔说：“这些是示意线。真实飞行还需要安排路线、保持间隔、处理天气变化，不能只看一条发光的线。”

**English**

Colored lines in the simulation helped An'an understand the routes. “These are illustrations,” Uncle Zhao explained. “Real flights need planned routes, safe separation, and responses to changing weather, not just a glowing line.”

### 第 15 页

体验结束，赵叔叔带他们看立体交通模型：“不同工具各有适合的任务，还需要车站、起降场和许多工作人员合作。”安安点点头：“未来城市，也要大家一起照顾！”

**English**

After the simulation, Uncle Zhao showed them a model of layered transport. “Different vehicles suit different jobs. Stations, landing sites, and many workers must cooperate.” An'an nodded. “A future city needs everyone to care for it too!”

### 第 16 页

夜幕降临，城市亮起了万家灯火。安安坐在家里的浅蓝色汽车后排，怀抱小熊平安回家。在书桌前，她拿起彩笔，认真画下了今天见过的无人机、高铁小飞龙、大客机、航天卫星与空中出租车。她知道，这就是我们正在奔向的明天。

**English**

As night fell, thousands of city lights sparkled across the skyline. Sitting in the back seat of their light-blue car, An'an hugged Bear as they arrived home safely. At her desk, she picked up colored pencils, lovingly drawing the drone, bullet train, airliner, satellite, and air taxi she had seen today. She knew this was the bright tomorrow we are building together.

### 亲子共读

共读目标：认识电动垂直起降概念，区分固定翼、旋翼与不同推进构型；了解无需长距离滑跑仍需要合适起降场，并能区分概念动画和真实城市运行。

家长提醒：展品、城市模型和模拟舱用于未来出行科普，并不表示任何楼顶都能起降、所有城市都已普及。旋翼仍有噪声，电池电力并非全生命周期零排放；实际运行受机型、场地、天气和批准条件约束。孩子只体验静止座舱，按工作人员要求系好安全带。

可以聊一聊：

- 不需要长跑道，为什么仍要有合适的起降场？
- 纯电飞行为什么仍要关心声音和电池？
- 地铁、汽车、高铁和航空器，各适合什么出行任务？

桌面小游戏：准备一张大开画纸，和孩子一起用蓝色、绿色与银色彩笔绘制一张“未来立体都市交通图”：画出地底的隧道线、地面的高铁与马路、天空中的彩虹空中航道，并在不同位置贴上自己折叠的纸飞机与小积木车，讲述属于自己的立体出行故事。

4–5 岁：找出静止旋翼、座舱和安全带，分清展厅体验与真实飞行。

6–8 岁：比较不同交通工具的用途与局限，为未来城市画出换乘点、起降场与合理间隔。
